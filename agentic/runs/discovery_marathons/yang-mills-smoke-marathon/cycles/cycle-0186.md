# Reflection-Positivity Closed-Cone Topology: Wilson Boundary Cross-Ratio Test

## Candidate Lemma

Let \(W_1,\ldots,W_n\) be a finite family in the locked positive-time gauge-invariant Wilson observable algebra, and let \(T_\delta W_i\) be mirror-compatible collar representatives supported at distance \(\delta>0\) from the reflection plane. Suppose fixed-collar RG convergence gives positive OS matrices \(N^{(\delta)}\) and the boundary trace limit has no additive contact term but may have a multiplicative anomaly \(\Gamma\): \(N_{ij}=\Gamma_{ij}M_{ij}\), where \(M_{ij}=S(\Theta W_i,W_j)\) is the intended target OS matrix. Then the collar positivity bridge is valid for arbitrary finite OS matrices exactly when \(\Gamma^{[-1]}=(1/\Gamma_{ij})\) is a positive Schur multiplier. If, in addition, \(\Gamma\) itself is an OS-positive boundary kernel with nonzero entries, this forces the normalized anomaly to be rank-one, equivalently all logarithmic four-point cross-ratios vanish. Thus any nonzero Wilson boundary log cross-ratio is a same-theory falsifier, not merely a removable normalization defect.

## Definitions

- source object: regulated and then continuum fixed-collar Schwinger matrices \(N^{(\delta)}_{ij}=S(\Theta T_\delta W_i,T_\delta W_j)\) obtained from reflection-positive lattice/RG Yang-Mills observables in the locked gauge-invariant algebra.
- target object: intended OS-positive continuum Schwinger matrix \(M_{ij}=S(\Theta W_i,W_j)\) on the uncollared positive-time Wilson observables, prior to adding any boundary counterterm or changing Wilson-loop renormalization.
- controlled interface: regulated reflection-positive Schwinger forms -> OS-positive continuum Schwinger functions, now refined to the two-step passage fixed collar \(\delta>0\) followed by trace limit \(\delta\downarrow0\) for every finite reflected Wilson OS matrix.
- same-theory condition: the collar trace may identify \(T_\delta W_i\to W_i\) only through the already chosen individual renormalizations of the locked observable algebra; a pair-dependent factor \(\Gamma_{ij}\) is allowed only if it is an invertible diagonal congruence already present in that algebra.
- Schur reciprocal multiplier: for a nonzero matrix \(\Gamma\), \(\Gamma^{[-1]}\circ N\) must be positive semidefinite for every positive semidefinite finite matrix \(N\) arising at the interface; the universal finite-matrix version asks this for all \(N\ge0\).
- log cross-ratio certificate: for positive real entries, \(K_{ijkl}=\log\Gamma_{ij}+\log\Gamma_{kl}-\log\Gamma_{il}-\log\Gamma_{kj}\); rank-one diagonal congruence is equivalent to \(K_{ijkl}=0\) for all indices.

## Assumptions

- Fixed-collar reflected Wilson probes are valid test observables for the RG cutoff-removal topology: for each \(\delta>0\), \(S_{a,L}(\Theta T_\delta W_i,T_\delta W_j)\to S(\Theta T_\delta W_i,T_\delta W_j)\).
- For each fixed \(\delta>0\), reflection positivity holds before cutoff removal and therefore the fixed-collar continuum matrix \(N^{(\delta)}\) is positive semidefinite.
- Diagonal trace continuity has been locked: \(S(\Theta T_\delta W_i,T_\delta W_i)\to S(\Theta W_i,W_i)\) after the permitted individual Wilson-loop renormalizations.
- The remaining boundary trace defect is multiplicative and finite on the chosen family: \(N_{ij}=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}N^{(\delta)}_{ij}=\Gamma_{ij}M_{ij}\), with \(\Gamma_{ij}\ne0\).
- Any additive positive boundary contact has zero diagonal after diagonal trace locking, hence vanishes by Cauchy-Schwarz; if an additive nonpositive contact remains, it is separately a falsifier of OS-compatible boundary OPE.
- The extra rank-one conclusion assumes \(\Gamma\) is itself a positive boundary kernel, as would be expected if the anomaly is generated by an auxiliary reflection-positive boundary degree of freedom rather than by an indefinite subtraction.

## Equations

- regulated fixed-collar positivity: `Q_{a,L}^{(\delta)}(c)=\sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j\,S_{a,L}(\Theta T_\delta W_i,T_\delta W_j)\ge0`. Valid when: Valid for each fixed collar separation \(\delta>0\) when the regulated Wilson action/measure is reflection-positive and the collar observables lie strictly in positive Euclidean time.
- fixed-collar continuum matrix: `N^{(\delta)}_{ij}=\lim_{a\downarrow0,L\uparrow\infty}S_{a,L}(\Theta T_\delta W_i,T_\delta W_j),\qquad N^{(\delta)}\ge0`. Valid when: Valid when the RG compactness/convergence topology controls these reflected Wilson pairings at fixed positive distance from the reflection plane.
- multiplicative trace anomaly: `N_{ij}=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}N^{(\delta)}_{ij}=\Gamma_{ij}M_{ij},\qquad M_{ij}=S(\Theta W_i,W_j)`. Valid when: Valid only after additive contacts have been excluded or shown to vanish and after individual Wilson-loop renormalizations have already been locked as part of the same theory.
- Schur reciprocal transfer: `M=\Gamma^{[-1]}\circ N,\qquad \Gamma^{[-1]}_{ij}=1/\Gamma_{ij}`. Valid when: This transfers positivity from every positive collar-limit matrix \(N\) to the intended target matrix \(M\) exactly when \(\Gamma^{[-1]}\) is a positive semidefinite kernel, by the Schur product theorem and its finite converse.
- normalized two-by-two obstruction: `\Gamma_{ii}=\Gamma_{jj}=1,\quad 0<\beta=\Gamma_{ij}<1,\quad N_t=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\ t&1\end{pmatrix},\quad \beta<t<1\ \Longrightarrow\ \det(\Gamma^{[-1]}\circ N_t)=1-(t/\beta)^2<0`. Valid when: Valid as a universal finite-matrix falsifier showing that a normalized damping anomaly \(\beta<1\) cannot justify a theory-independent positivity bridge unless the actual Yang-Mills matrices avoid the bad positive direction for an additional reason.
- log cross-ratio rank-one criterion: `K_{ijkl}=\log\Gamma_{ij}+\log\Gamma_{kl}-\log\Gamma_{il}-\log\Gamma_{kj};\qquad K_{ijkl}=0\ \forall i,j,k,l\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Gamma_{ij}=r_i r_j`. Valid when: Valid for strictly positive real anomaly entries; with phases the corresponding condition is that the normalized entries have modulus one and form a coboundary phase.

## Attempted Derivation

- Start from regulated reflection positivity: for every fixed collar distance \(\delta>0\), the finite matrix \(S_{a,L}(\Theta T_\delta W_i,T_\delta W_j)\) is positive semidefinite.
- Use the RG-to-OS fixed-collar convergence hypothesis to pass \(a\downarrow0,L\uparrow\infty\), obtaining \(N^{(\delta)}\ge0\). This is the already valid closed-cone part, provided the topology controls reflected Wilson probes.
- Take \(\delta\downarrow0\). Diagonal trace continuity and positive-contact Cauchy-Schwarz eliminate positive additive contacts, leaving the sharp obstruction in a multiplicative anomaly \(N=\Gamma\circ M\).
- The intended OS matrix is therefore \(M=\Gamma^{[-1]}\circ N\). If \(\Gamma^{[-1]}\ge0\) as a finite kernel, the Schur product theorem gives \(M\ge0\) from \(N\ge0\). Conversely, if the bridge is supposed to work for arbitrary positive finite collar matrices, testing matrix units and two-by-two positive matrices forces \(\Gamma^{[-1]}\) to be positive.
- Assume also that the boundary anomaly \(\Gamma\) is positive and diagonally normalized. For every pair, positivity of \(\Gamma\) gives \(|\Gamma_{ij}|\le1\); positivity of \(\Gamma^{[-1]}\) gives \(|1/\Gamma_{ij}|\le1\). Hence \(|\Gamma_{ij}|=1\). A positive matrix with all normalized entries of modulus one is rank-one, so \(\Gamma_{ij}=r_i\overline r_j\) up to phase, and in the positive real case \(\Gamma_{ij}=r_i r_j\).
- Taking logarithms in the positive real case gives \(\log\Gamma_{ij}=\log r_i+\log r_j\), hence every log cross-ratio vanishes. Conversely, vanishing of all such cross-ratios reconstructs \(r_i\) from one reference row, proving rank-one diagonal congruence.

Blocking gap: The finite-dimensional Schur/cross-ratio derivation is valid, but it is not yet a Yang-Mills proof because the analytic boundary trace theorem for reflected Wilson collars is missing. We still need to prove that the actual continuum limit has either no multiplicative anomaly or only a rank-one anomaly after the locked individual renormalizations; equivalently, we need a real RG/OPE estimate showing all Wilson boundary log cross-ratios vanish.

## Falsifiers

- falsifier: a four-loop reflected Wilson collar calculation with nonzero \(K_{ik;jl}=\log(\Gamma_{ij}\Gamma_{kl}/\Gamma_{il}\Gamma_{kj})\), showing a pair-dependent boundary anomaly not absorbable as individual Wilson renormalization.
- falsifier: a two-loop normalized damping anomaly \(0<\beta<1\) together with an actual collar-limit off diagonal \(t\) satisfying \(\beta<t<1\), giving \(\det M=1-(t/\beta)^2<0\).
- falsifier: a boundary cusp or hairpin anomalous dimension depending jointly on both loops at the reflection plane rather than as a sum of one-loop terms.
- falsifier: an additive boundary contact with zero diagonal and nonzero off diagonal, which cannot be a positive contact and therefore contradicts OS-compatible trace continuity.
- falsifier: an attempted proof that absorbs \(\Gamma_{ij}\) by changing the Wilson-loop algebra after cutoff removal; that may yield a positive modified theory but not a valid same-theory OS bridge for Yang-Mills.
- falsifier: RG compactness estimates that converge on separated collars but fail to control the four-entry cross-ratio limit as collars approach the reflection plane.

## Learning

Cycle 186 converts the Wilson boundary obstruction into a computable invariant: the logarithmic cross-ratio of the multiplicative anomaly. The finite-dimensional theorem is valid and sharp: collar positivity transfers to the intended OS matrix through a multiplicative boundary defect exactly by a reciprocal Schur multiplier, and under a positive boundary-kernel hypothesis this leaves only rank-one diagonal congruences. Therefore the remaining Yang-Mills problem can be attacked by calculating or bounding \(K_{ik;jl}\) for reflected Wilson collars; a nonzero value is a same-theory falsifier, while \(K=0\) on a dense Wilson core would strongly promote the OS bridge.
