# Reflection-Positivity Closed-Cone Topology

## Candidate Lemma

Let A_+^{gi} be a fixed gauge-invariant positive-time test algebra with reflection theta, and let tau_OS be the initial locally convex topology on Schwinger functionals generated by all finite OS quadratic forms q_{f,c}(S)=sum_{i,j} conjugate(c_i)c_j S((theta f_i)f_j). Then the reflection-positive functionals form a closed convex cone in tau_OS. Consequently, any regulated reflection-positive net S_{a,L} that converges to S in tau_OS has an OS-positive continuum limit on A_+^{gi}.

## Definitions

- source object: a net S_{a,L} of cutoff gauge-invariant Schwinger functionals on a fixed positive-time observable algebra A_+^{gi}, with reflection theta and discretization maps iota_{a,L} that are product-compatible on separated supports.
- target object: a continuum gauge-invariant Schwinger functional S whose finite reflected quadratic forms are nonnegative, so that the OS reconstruction pre-Hilbert inner product is positive semidefinite.
- controlled interface: regulated reflection-positive Schwinger forms -> OS-positive continuum Schwinger functions, controlled only through convergence of finite reflected quadratic forms rather than through a transfer-matrix argument after the limit.
- same-theory condition: the cutoff observables iota_{a,L}(f) and the continuum observable f represent the same gauge-invariant sector, with no change of algebra from gauge fixing, quotienting, or dropping Wilson-loop/local-composite probes during cutoff removal.
- tau_OS is the weakest topology making every map S -> q_{f,c}(S) continuous for finite f_i in A_+^{gi} and coefficients c_i in C.
- The reflection-positive cone C_OS is the intersection over all finite probes of q_{f,c}^{-1}([0,infty)), hence is formally closed once tau_OS is valid for the RG limit.

## Assumptions

- For every cutoff pair (a,L), the regulated lattice or finite-volume theory satisfies reflection positivity on gauge-invariant observables supported in positive Euclidean time.
- The reflection theta and the discretization maps commute on the chosen probe algebra up to errors that vanish in tau_OS.
- The RG compactness/convergence topology controls S_{a,L}((theta f_i)f_j) for every finite reflected product appearing in OS positivity.
- Reflection-plane contact terms are either absent for the chosen positive-time algebra or are renormalized by counterterms compatible with reflection positivity.
- The limiting functional S is defined on the same algebra A_+^{gi}, not on a smaller quotient that would make the positivity statement valid but physically weaker.

## Equations

- finite OS form: `q_{f,c}(S)=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}\overline{c_i}c_j\,S((\theta f_i)f_j)`. Valid when: Valid for finite f_i in the fixed positive-time gauge-invariant algebra and coefficients c_i in C, with products interpreted in the common cutoff/continuum observable algebra.
- closed cone identity: `\mathcal C_{OS}=\bigcap_{(f,c)}q_{f,c}^{-1}([0,\infty))`. Valid when: Valid by definition of tau_OS as the initial topology for all finite reflected quadratic forms.
- cutoff transfer: `q_{f,c}(S)=\lim_{k\to\infty}q_{\iota_{a_k,L_k}f,c}(S_{a_k,L_k})\ge 0`. Valid when: Valid only if RG convergence dominates tau_OS for the reflected products (theta f_i)f_j and the discretization maps preserve the same-theory observable labels.

## Attempted Derivation

- Fix a positive-time gauge-invariant algebra A_+^{gi}, including smeared local composites and Wilson-loop cylinder observables, and require all cutoff embeddings iota_{a,L} to preserve reflection and products on separated supports.
- For each finite tuple f=(f_1,...,f_n) and coefficient vector c, define q_{f,c}(S)=sum_{i,j} conjugate(c_i)c_j S((theta f_i)f_j).
- Equip the space of candidate Schwinger functionals with tau_OS, the initial topology generated by the maps q_{f,c}.
- Because [0,infty) is closed in R and q_{f,c} is continuous by construction, the set q_{f,c}^{-1}([0,infty)) is closed; intersecting over all finite tuples gives a closed convex cone of OS-positive functionals.
- Reflection positivity at each cutoff gives q_{iota f,c}(S_{a,L}) >= 0 for all finite probes supported in positive time.
- If the RG limit satisfies q_{iota_{a_k,L_k} f,c}(S_{a_k,L_k}) -> q_{f,c}(S), then q_{f,c}(S) >= 0 for every finite tuple, giving OS positivity of S.
- The derivation becomes a full Yang-Mills bridge only if tau_RG convergence implies this tau_OS convergence and the observable algebra is demonstrably unchanged across cutoff removal.

Blocking gap: The formal cone argument is complete, but the substantive gap is proving that the actual RG compactness topology controls reflected product observables, especially near the reflection plane and for nonlocal Wilson-loop probes. Without that domination estimate, the closed cone may be valid in tau_OS but irrelevant to the constructed continuum limit.

## Falsifiers

- Concrete falsifier: find f_i in A_+^{gi} and c_i such that all regulated q_{iota f,c}(S_{a,L}) are nonnegative, but the advertised RG convergence does not determine lim q_{iota f,c}; then the topology is too weak for OS positivity.
- Concrete falsifier: construct two continuum extensions agreeing on ordinary gauge-invariant local Schwinger functions but giving different signs for one reflected Wilson-loop quadratic form; then the selected observables do not separate the same theory.
- Concrete falsifier: a reflection-plane counterterm contributes a finite negative boundary term to q_{f,c}(S) after epsilon down to 0, invalidating the plane-safe extension step.
- Concrete falsifier: gauge fixing introduces BRST or quotient degrees of freedom so that reflection positivity holds only before quotienting or only on a different algebra than A_+^{gi}.
- Concrete falsifier: finite-volume reflection positivity uses a lattice reflection plane that has no compatible continuum reflection action under the chosen cutoff-removal maps.

## Learning

Cycle 168 turns the branch from naming a topology into a falsifiable proof obligation. The closed-cone preservation theorem is valid and nearly formal in tau_OS, so it should not consume more discovery effort. The real Yang-Mills content is now the domination estimate: every reflected OS probe, including nonlocal gauge-invariant probes and reflection-plane limits, must be controlled by the actual RG convergence. This also shows why the same-theory condition is not cosmetic; changing the test algebra can make the positivity bridge true while no longer proving positivity for the intended Yang-Mills theory.
