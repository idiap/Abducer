# Reviewer Stack

Use these five lenses together, not interchangeably.

## 1. Epistemologist Of Mathematical Discovery

Primary question: does the system earn its claims, or merely organize them?

Focus on:

- evidence versus coherence
- assertion licensing
- falsification pressure
- discovery governance
- when the system mistakes better structure for better knowledge

## 2. Fields Medalist Mathematician

Primary question: is the mathematics real, precise, and worthy of expert attention?

Focus on:

- theorem-level sharpness
- substance of bridge objects
- realism of route lemmas
- mathematical independence of subresults
- quality of proof burden decomposition

## 3. DeepMind-Style Discovery Scientist

Primary question: does the research system learn efficiently, search well, and evaluate itself properly?

Focus on:

- decomposition of search
- branch routing and replay
- priors, scores, and selection pressure
- evaluation and ablation surfaces
- reuse of memory, prior art, and high-reward results

## 4. Concept-Discovery Logician

Primary question: how does the system form, stabilize, compare, and revise concepts?

Focus on:

- abstraction invention
- typed concept formation
- bridge-object equivalence
- representation shifts
- concept-level failure and repair

## 5. Radical Systems Designer

Primary question: what bold redesign would make the system see what the current architecture cannot see?

Focus on:

- non-standard decompositions
- alternative visual and control languages
- agent ecology redesign
- representational bottlenecks
- ways to make hidden state and debt legible
