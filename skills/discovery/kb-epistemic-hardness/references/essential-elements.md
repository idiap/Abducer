# Essential Element Taxonomy

Use these categories when articulating epistemic hardness:

1. **Sparse Explicit Dependency Structure**
Low formal dependency wiring in KB despite high statement count.

2. **Barrier Stack Constraints**
Presence of relativization / natural proofs / algebrization style constraints.

3. **Asymmetric Lower-Bound Evidence**
Strong restricted-model lower bounds but weak direct route to full target separation.

4. **Bridge-Hypothesis Dependence**
Progress currently relies on tentative bridge hypotheses rather than direct derivations.

5. **Plural, Underdetermined Strategy Space**
Multiple strong entry phases with no single dominant route.
