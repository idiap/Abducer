# Named-workflow trace expert review

Review status: implementation gate checklist. A reviewer records pass/fail and a short evidence note for every generated workflow report and Route-A profile report.

## Shared questions

- [ ] The first view identifies workflow, run, target, profile, current disposition, and the lens’s expert question.
- [ ] The route distinguishes required, conditional, validator, advisory, and specialist roles.
- [ ] Control execution is visually distinct from substantive inference.
- [ ] At least one inference exposes a question, typed premises, operator, public warrant, conclusion, uncertainty, and scope.
- [ ] Supporting, refuting, constraining, and background evidence are not presented as equivalent.
- [ ] A realistic rejected alternative, failure, demotion, or minority finding is visible without opening raw JSON.
- [ ] Artifact integrity, claim epistemic status, and workflow disposition can disagree without visual ambiguity.
- [ ] Authority exercised, authority absent, prohibitions, obligations, gates, and handoffs are inspectable.
- [ ] Trace gaps and justified conditional N/A steps are distinct.
- [ ] Filtering, keyboard navigation, narrow layout, and filesystem-only operation remain usable.

## Workflow-specific contrasts

- Evidence-to-route: provenance and assertion license survive normalization; hub bias, regime changes, and falsifiers can demote a route.
- Conceptual deepening: candidate identity remains blind through adjudication; rejection and later audit disclosure are ordered correctly.
- Theorem delivery: the exact statement, obligation DAG, failed lemma/countermodel split, circularity prohibition, and proof gate remain visible.
- Contribution cultivation: correctness, novelty, alignment, mechanism evidence, enablement, and paper readiness are separate judgments.
- Manuscript elaboration: unresolved markers remain obligations; finite, asymptotic, and continuum claims are never collapsed.
- Deep review: independent reviews and minority findings survive meta-synthesis; the workflow has no proof-acceptance or release authority.
- Route-A: specialist fan-out and provenance, symbolic/CAS audit, same-theory binding, endpoint and finite-margin failures, join, integration, and common validation tail are visible.

## Release decision

The generic report remains opt-in until all checklist items pass for the seven reports, cross-workflow handoffs are exercised, semantic coverage is computed from the same validated packet shown in HTML, and the frozen main-workflow compatibility suite remains green.
