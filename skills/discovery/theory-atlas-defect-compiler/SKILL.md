# Theory Atlas Defect Compiler

Use this skill before `categorical-concept-forger` when the pressure is not yet a sufficiently typed categorical problem. Treat the attached higher-abductive archive only as the provenance of the meaning-profile, repair, reflection, defect, and redescription vocabularies. Never import or execute its runtime.

## Workflow

1. Freeze each theory fragment as a signature, axioms, models, observables, scope, evidence references, and all nine source-bound meaning dimensions.
2. Type horizontal and vertical relations completely. Type partial relations without silently totalizing them; list every unmapped source symbol.
3. Build diagrams from fragment objects, typed morphisms, relation components, and parallel-path higher cells. A categorical word without endpoints and an executable obligation is a metaphor, not a registered defect.
4. Attempt the six repairs in order: lexical, unit/notation, definitional extension, known equivalence, conservative interpretation, then scope restriction. Do not diagnose an essential defect while a cheaper repair succeeds or is merely untried.
5. Apply all eight philosophical lenses. Make each lens change at least one commitment, question, and failure mode rather than restating the source.
6. Sweep all twelve redescription operators. Mark each `applied` with candidate references or `not_applicable` with a reason.
7. Register only typed defects and compile structure-specific finite obligations. Cover factorization, finite representability, descent/gluing, pushout, adjunction/naturality, and a lifting obstruction.
8. Propose a representation mutation only after repeated failures of the same structured defect. State the source and proposed grammars, total typed migration map, information loss, new expressivity, and pending human-only acceptance gate.
9. Replay both exact and bounded approximate abstract-to-concrete return squares. Require unique square identities and coverage of every registered completion candidate. Hand changed structures and unresolved burdens back to the physics route.
10. Validate through `skills.common.theory_atlas_defect_compiler.validate_theory_atlas_defect_packet` before categorical forging.

## Output and Authority

Emit one strict v1 packet. Successful finite replay establishes only that the local example satisfies the declared check. It does not prove a universal property, justify homotopy or univalence, establish truth or conceptual value, deliver a physics theorem, or authorize investment. Send surviving candidates to `categorical-concept-forger`; send identity/equivalence pressure to `hott-equivalence-coherence-forger`; retain exact physics return-square burdens for the physics bridge.

## Failure and Demotion

Reject symbol-type swaps, partial-relation totalization, nonparallel higher cells, omitted or reordered repairs, restatement-only reflection, unregistered completion vocabulary, stale finite replays, premature representation mutation, failed error bounds, and any proof/value promotion. Preserve these as reusable negative structural evidence.
