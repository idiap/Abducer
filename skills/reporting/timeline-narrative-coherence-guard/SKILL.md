# Timeline Narrative Coherence Guard

## Workflow

Before writing or revising a timeline-derived paper:

1. Name the bounded paper subgoal in one sentence.
2. State the coherent narrative thesis that the paper can honestly defend now.
3. Build a narrative spine from problem pressure to mathematical object, theorem/example, transfer debt, and contribution claim.
4. Classify every candidate detail as body, appendix/future work, or quarantine.
5. Admit only details that strengthen the theorem, worked example, transfer certificate, prior-art boundary, or honest contribution claim.
6. State the decisive next work and the exact conditions under which continued exploration should stop, demote, or pivot.

## Current Yang-Mills Paper Lock

For the active Yang-Mills timeline, the paper goal is the finite Wilson/OS Schur-Feshbach obstruction note. The paper must not become a chronological digest of the whole discovery loop. It should converge on either proving the finite tangent-cone theorem or computing the first concrete \(H,K\) reflected Wilson collar/hairpin case, with a same-theory transfer certificate as the explicit boundary.

## Epistemic Discipline

Separate paper value from discovery activity. More cycles are not evidence of paper progress unless they improve a claim-bearing object, proof, worked example, transfer estimate, or literature boundary.

Do not stop prematurely when a branch is still sharpening a theorem, coefficient extraction, or falsifiable same-theory transfer obstruction. Do stop or demote when successive passes merely restate the obstruction ladder, add chronology, or introduce speculative branches that do not change the paper claim.

## Output Contract

Return: bounded subgoal, narrative thesis, narrative spine, included-material policy, excluded-detail policy, detail budget, decisive next work, stop/demote criteria, do-not-stop-prematurely criteria, coherence checks, epistemic status, and validation handoff.

## Failure/Demotion

Demote the paper draft if it cannot name its subgoal, if the body is organized as run chronology rather than argument, if details do not support the theorem/example/transfer/claim spine, or if the conclusion implies a stronger result than the validated subgoal.

## Validation Handoff

The timeline-paper validator must find a `paper_narrative_goal_lock` in the JSON artifact and a visible "Paper Narrative Goal Lock" section in the Markdown and TeX outputs.
