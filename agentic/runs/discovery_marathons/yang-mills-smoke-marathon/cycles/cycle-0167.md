# Reflection-Positivity Closed-Cone Topology

## Candidate Lemma

Let \(\mathcal A_+^{\mathrm{gi}}\) be a cutoff-independent positive-time gauge-invariant test algebra with reflection \(\Theta\). If the RG cutoff-removal topology \(\tau_{RG}\) on Schwinger functionals is at least as strong as the initial topology generated by all finite reflected quadratic forms \(S\mapsto \sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j S((\Theta F_i)F_j)\), then every \(\tau_{RG}\)-limit of regulated reflection-positive Schwinger functionals is OS reflection positive on \(\mathcal A_+^{\mathrm{gi}}\).

## Definitions

- source object: a net \(S_{a,L}\) of regulated gauge-invariant Euclidean Schwinger functionals on one fixed algebra \(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{gi}}\), each satisfying finite reflection-positivity inequalities on \(\mathcal A_+^{\mathrm{gi}}\).
- target object: continuum OS-positive Schwinger functions \(S\) on \(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{gi}}\), suitable for OS reconstruction of a physical Hilbert space after the remaining OS axioms are separately verified.
- controlled interface: regulated reflection-positive Schwinger forms -> OS-positive continuum Schwinger functions, mediated only by convergence of reflected finite quadratic forms.
- same-theory condition: the reflected test algebra, reflection map, renormalized observable labels, and gauge-invariant quotient are independent of \((a,L)\); no change of theory is hidden in gauge fixing, completion, or counterterm-dependent observable redefinition.

## Assumptions

- For every finite \(F_1,\dots,F_N\in\mathcal A_+^{\mathrm{gi}}\) and coefficients \(c\in\mathbb C^N\), the map \(S\mapsto Q_S(F,c)\) is \(\tau_{RG}\)-continuous.
- Each regulated functional is reflection positive: \(Q_{S_{a,L}}(F,c)\ge0\) for all finite reflected tests.
- The continuum candidate \(S\) is the same \(\tau_{RG}\)-limit used to prove the distributional Schwinger functions, not a separately extracted OS-positive subsequential limit.
- Products \((\Theta F_i)F_j\) remain in the distributional domain controlled by the multiscale estimates.

## Equations

- initial OS seminorm probes: `p_{F,c}(S):=\left|\sum_{i,j=1}^{N}\overline c_i c_j\,S((\Theta F_i)F_j)\right|`. Valid when: \(F_i\in\mathcal A_+^{\mathrm{gi}}\), \(c_i\in\mathbb C\), and the product observable is in the common gauge-invariant test domain.
- cone closure: `S_{a,L}\xrightarrow{\tau_{RG}}S,\quad \tau_{RG}\succeq\tau_{OS}^{\mathrm{quad}},\quad Q_{S_{a,L}}(F,c)\ge0\ \forall(a,L)\ \Longrightarrow\ Q_S(F,c)\ge0`. Valid when: The convergence is along the same cutoff-removal net and each finite reflected quadratic form is continuous in the chosen topology.
- OS null-space inner product: `\langle [F],[G]\rangle_{OS}:=S((\Theta F)G),\qquad \mathcal N=\{F: S((\Theta F)F)=0\}`. Valid when: The preceding closure has made the sesquilinear form positive semidefinite on the continuum positive-time algebra.

## Attempted Derivation

- Fix \(F_1,\dots,F_N\in\mathcal A_+^{\mathrm{gi}}\) and \(c\in\mathbb C^N\). At each cutoff define \(Q_{a,L}(F,c)=\sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j S_{a,L}((\Theta F_i)F_j)\).
- Use regulated reflection positivity to obtain \(Q_{a,L}(F,c)\ge0\) for every cutoff in the net.
- Assume \(S_{a,L}\to S\) in \(\tau_{RG}\) and that \(\tau_{RG}\) dominates the finite reflected-form topology. Then \(Q_{a,L}(F,c)\to Q_S(F,c)\).
- Since the closed half-line \([0,\infty)\subset\mathbb R\) is closed, the limit satisfies \(Q_S(F,c)\ge0\). This proves continuum reflection positivity for the fixed finite family.
- Quantify over all finite families in the locked positive-time algebra. The OS inner product \(S((\Theta F)G)\) is therefore positive semidefinite and has a valid null quotient completion, provided the other OS axioms are available.

Blocking gap: The nontrivial missing proof is not convex closedness itself; it is the domination claim \(\tau_{RG}\succeq\tau_{OS}^{\mathrm{quad}}\) for the actual Balaban-style compactness topology, especially for reflected products that approach the time-zero plane and for renormalized composite gauge-invariant observables.

## Falsifiers

- Topology falsifier: construct a sequence of regulated Schwinger functionals converging in the multiscale distribution topology while a reflected quadratic form has a negative or nonconvergent continuum value.
- Algebra falsifier: show that the gauge-invariant positive-time algebra used at cutoff depends on \((a,L)\) through renormalization choices so no single \(\mathcal A_+^{\mathrm{gi}}\) receives all positivity inequalities.
- Reflection-plane falsifier: find a contact counterterm supported at \(t=0\) that is invisible to separated-time RG bounds but changes \(S((\Theta F)F)\).
- Gauge-quotient falsifier: demonstrate that positivity holds before imposing the continuum Gauss-law or gauge-invariant quotient but fails after the quotient/completion used for the claimed physical theory.
- Subsequence falsifier: the only OS-positive continuum object is obtained by an extraction different from the one yielding the claimed Schwinger distributions, so the bridge does not preserve the same theory.

## Learning

The real bridge lemma is almost tautological once the right topology is named: reflection-positive functionals form a closed convex cone in the initial topology of finite OS quadratic forms. The discovery pressure has therefore moved to two precise obligations: prove the RG compactness topology is valid for those reflected probes, and prove the locked gauge-invariant observable algebra is the same theory before and after cutoff removal. This separates a formal closed-cone preservation step from the substantive Yang-Mills analytic estimate still missing.
