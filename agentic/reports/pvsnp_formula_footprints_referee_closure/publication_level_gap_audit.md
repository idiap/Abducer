## Self-Auditing RUP-CDCL Update

The carrier-independent audit route is now instantiated for one enriched
certificate format: `self_auditing_rup_cdcl_instantiation.md` proves that an
RUP-CDCL proof object with an internal label decoder, live-database
reconstruction, checker-visible RUP propagation witnesses, retained
deleted-clause support, and support-local auxiliary witnesses satisfies
\(\Cref{cor:carrier-independent-audit-upgrade}\) with linear audit budget.
This is a real proof-system bridge, but it is not a raw DIMACS/RUP/DRUP,
arbitrary CDCL, Frege, Extended Frege, IPS, or arbitrary compressed-proof lower
bound.

The append-only refinement `append_only_self_auditing_rup_cdcl.md` removes one
annotation layer: if the proof trace is deletion-free, the monotone
clause-addition log replaces retained deleted-clause support records.  This is
still not a raw RUP/DRUP or arbitrary CDCL theorem, because the checked decoder,
RUP witnesses, and support-locality audit remain part of the proof object.  The
prefix-typed refinement `prefix_typed_append_only_rup_cdcl.md` removes the
proof-internal decoder as well, but only when formula-label variables are public
typed syntax.  The boundary packet
`prefix_typed_rup_support_witness_necessity.md` shows that public label typing
and append-only RUP checking still do not certify completion-local auxiliary
support without a support-local witness or equivalent intrinsic invariant.  The
remaining C5/C10/C11 blocker is therefore narrower: supply an intrinsic support-locality invariant for touched auxiliary clauses, handle
deletion-bearing or untyped RUP-CDCL traces, or switch to the explicit
SAT-family/source route.

The new intrinsic-locality packet
`intrinsic_locality_prefix_typed_rup_cdcl.md` supplies that invariant as a
positive abstract proof-system condition: fixed public completion-stable
auxiliary rules with checker-computable support sets replace separate
support-local witness fields.  The remaining blocker is now external
recognizability: instantiate that rule discipline in a natural solver/proof
format, handle deletion-bearing or untyped traces, or switch to the explicit
SAT-family/source route.

1. **Proof-system extraction route**: the positive non-plain CDCL criterion is now met by the RUP-complete CDCL support normalizer, the carrier-specific namespace parsing route is standardized by the canonical-namespace RUP-CDCL corollary, the carrier-independent audit upgrade criterion states the exact extractor obligation that would remove the manifest, the self-auditing RUP-CDCL corollary instantiates it for an enriched proof format whose proof object carries the decoder and support audit, the append-only corollary removes retained deleted-clause support annotations in the deletion-free case, the prefix-typed append-only corollary replaces the proof-internal decoder with public typed formula-label syntax, the prefix-typed support-witness necessity proposition shows this still does not certify auxiliary support-locality by syntax alone, and the intrinsic-locality prefix-typed corollary supplies the exact fixed-rule condition that does.
