# Framework Paper Consistency Adjudicator

For conceptual-organization requests, follow [the shared conceptual inquiry contract](../framework-core-mechanism-curator/references/conceptual-inquiry-contract.md). Compare the actual body, title/abstract/introduction/conclusion, figure arrows, and blueprint against the reviewed 2.0.0 model. Reject an operator-first outline hidden under conceptual headings, omitted diagnostic exit, decorative six-lens prose, unification by erased differences, scalar or list-order selection, and conceptual preference presented as proof. Verify actual alternatives, reflection-induced change, plural comparison, and revision conditions. Native operator mappings remain supporting many-to-many annotations. Retain the prior seven-field occurrence review and complete frozen appendix coverage separately.

Read only the frozen paper bundle. Treat fluent prose as untrusted until its section sidecar and claim ledger agree.

1. Verify every consumed claim ID exists and is allowed in that section.
2. Compare contribution numbering, terminology, notation, metrics, citations, and epistemic tense across sections.
3. Reject novelty, comparison, efficacy, causality, proof, or generalization language without the required authority.
4. Require limitations and failure/stop paths to survive compression into abstract and conclusion.
5. Emit findings with `fixed`, `deferred`, or `rejected` disposition and affected claim IDs.

Run `scripts/adjudicate_paper.py` before assembly and again on the assembled bundle.

For the optional dependent-type paper lane, manually reconcile binder/index names,
context assumptions, equality notation and native-versus-proposed status across the
model, section and claim ledger. A native nominal assessment and a proposed indexed
assessment must not silently share the same guarantee. If a categorical companion
is present, compare only the declared correspondence; require separate support for
an equivalence or semantic preservation claim. Record this review separately from
the fixed-section script's verdict.

Review every workflow example against its defining operation and actual values. Reject
copied generic narratives, administrative substitutes, invented observed results and
formal notation that does not match the case. Require an informative outcome, including
what a failed route rules out or changes. Reconcile all scoped workflows and child-fragment
claims with the parent section owner; coverage does not follow from example count alone.

## Mandatory step explanation gate

Read [the shared step explanation contract](../framework-paper-workflow-appendix-writer/references/step-explanation-contract.md) for occurrence-level paper revisions.

Review all seven explanatory questions at every step. Reject example discontinuities, unsupported native algorithms, detached assessments and generic signature restatements. Record per-workflow rationale and remaining source gaps; bind review to content digests.

## Recursive goals and workflow relationships

When this explanation is requested, read [the shared recursive-goals contract](../framework-core-mechanism-curator/references/recursive-goals-and-workflow-relations.md). Trace child-to-parent sufficiency, stopping scope, surviving OR alternatives, revision dependencies and complete workflow relationships across prose, tables and arrows. Reject budget-as-truth, child-completion-as-parent-proof, and editorial links presented as executed paths. This is a manual authoring extension, not a new fixed-runner schema or stage.
