# Literature-Conditioned Invention Forger

Use this skill at the generative hinge of a theory-first discovery cycle. Its
job is to let the newest bounded literature perturb conceptual invention
without allowing literature consensus to close the search space or pretending
that a digest association establishes creativity or benefit.

## Reuse before adding machinery

Reuse these existing capabilities in order:

1. Use frontier-literature-assimilator to obtain a bounded current snapshot,
   claim provenance, warrants, corrections, supersessions, retractions, and
   coverage limits.
2. Use the literature snapshot capture bridge and conceptual ancestry v3
   projection to keep raw capture bytes, semantic snapshot bytes, and the
   exact bridge distinct and replayable.
3. Use high-risk-hypothesis-forger for aggressive intuition, asymmetric
   upside, explicit risk, corrections, and falsifiers.
4. Use the receipt-backed research-worker boundary for process-local
   authorship; never accept caller-authored inventions or family labels as if
   the worker produced them.
5. Use parallel-hypothesis-portfolio only after the pre-taxonomy invention
   packet and independent family analysis are sealed.
6. Use reflective-conceptual-discovery and its six full-core lenses after
   invention. Experiments and proof remain descendants of committed theory.

The implementation surface is
agentic.discovery.literature_conditioned_invention. Prefer
run_registered_literature_conditioned_invention for production and
validate_literature_conditioned_invention_execution for every reopen.

The current v1 worker is an authored structural-atlas control. Label it
`operator_instantiated_from_authored_atlas`; process execution and target
grounding do not make it a proposal-field-free or genuinely creative
generator. A discovery-grade run must additionally use the high-risk forger's
parallel provider/author lanes and keep the atlas run as the matched control.

## Mandatory causal order

Enforce this order exactly:

1. Reopen the complete retained raw capture, bounded-current bundle, semantic
   bridge, semantic snapshot, and derived literature projection from their
   exact unequal bytes and digests.
2. Recheck target/run/state identity, source versions, correction indexes,
   cutoff ordering, and bounded currentness at the governed consumption time.
   Repeat after lock, after worker completion, before commit, and on any cache
   replay; retain the clock-attestation null unless a separate trusted-time
   authority exists.
3. Seal one request containing the target pressure, root contradictions,
   protected successes, exact semantic claims and spans, provenance, warrants,
   assimilation deltas, corrections, supersessions, retractions, and coverage
   limits.
4. Withhold the known conceptual taxonomy by commitment only.
5. Invoke the registered high-risk worker with the sealed request. Do not
   supply raw inventions, expected answers, corner names, family labels,
   rankings, or a preferred theory.
6. Preserve every authored intervention, discriminating prediction,
   correction, countermodel, falsifier, and physics-facing return.
7. First replay family types structurally from exact intervention structures
   and resolved target paths. Call this separately replayed verification, not
   cognitive or semantic independence. For discovery-grade family breadth,
   use a separately configured assessor that receives no worker labels and
   reconstructs mechanisms, interventions, predictions, corrections,
   falsifiers, and physics returns from complete content.
8. Seal the source invention freeze, independently derived family contracts,
   conditioned freeze, receipt, and bounded satisfiability result together.
9. Only then expose the known taxonomy to a separate classifier and begin
   isolated maturation.

## Aggressive intuitive search

Aim for six or more task-grounded structural families. Favor hypotheses that
change primitives, relations, explanatory direction, mediators, invariants,
obstruction locality, quotient structure, feedback, regimes, or carriers.
Every family must:

- cite exact semantic causal-input identities;
- resolve the target pressure and at least one protected success;
- state a concrete intervention rather than a family name;
- predict a decision-relevant change on a named target path;
- provide a countermodel-shaped first falsifier;
- state a correction that retains negative knowledge; and
- return to one exact physics or mathematics research action with a value
  condition and explicit nonclaim.

Do not force six cosmetic variants. The authored operation count or a Cartesian
product of available fields is not a satisfiability argument. If fewer than
six signatures are possible, require an executable bounded constraint model
and a verifier-replayed UNSAT core or countermodel over the actual target
constraints. A producer shortfall is not an impossibility proof.

## Literature intervention study

Before claiming that literature changed invention, run equal-authority,
equal-target counterfactual worlds for:

- current semantic literature;
- an exact historical projection;
- no literature;
- removal of each semantic causal input;
- claim substitution;
- correction;
- supersession; and
- retraction.

Rebuild each world through the same normalization and assimilation rules:
replace corrected claims and warrant bindings coherently, remove a retracted
claim from active anchors, and never treat a superseded claim as its missing
successor. Preserve equal seeds, opportunity, budgets, and authority.

For every world, preregister the expected semantic effect. Compare an
identity- and provenance-erased conceptual projection containing the actual
operation, target grounding, prediction, correction trigger, falsifier
outcome, and physics-facing action. Request, receipt, invention, source,
claim, and warrant identities—and correction labels alone—do not count as
effects. Report a changed conceptual signature or an explicit null; preserve
provenance-only churn as such. If every world is an explicit conceptual null,
the honest conclusion is that this generator did not use literature
generatively. This study establishes at most local semantic sensitivity;
comparative benefit requires the later publication-disabled held-out factorial
study.

## Parallel continuation

Once the packet is sealed, pass all admissible families to
parallel-hypothesis-portfolio with isolated namespaces and matched budgets.
Preserve dissent, failures, corrections, and high-upside wildcards through the
single-writer merge. Do not rank families by literature prestige, lexical
novelty, elegance, formalizability, proof proximity, or experiment outcome.

## Authority boundary

Keep all of the following null unless separately established: source truth,
global latestness, semantic entailment, novelty, creativity, conceptual merit,
literature benefit, evaluator independence, physics value, proof, theorem,
continuum result, spectral result, mass gap, and target truth.

The output may alter the candidate set only after canonical consumption. It
cannot directly select a theory, authorize a proof, schedule an experiment, or
promote a result.
