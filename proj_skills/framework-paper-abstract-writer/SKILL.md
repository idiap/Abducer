# Framework Paper Abstract Writer

For conceptual-organization requests, follow [the shared conceptual inquiry contract](../framework-core-mechanism-curator/references/conceptual-inquiry-contract.md). Summarize the accepted body's conceptual situation/tension, what inquiry changes, its reflective and plural character, and the conditions for revision. Preserve the diagnostic boundary and evidence posture without forcing every lens into the abstract. Native operator vocabulary supports the contribution; an eight-primitive list does not define it.

Consume only the blueprint, claim ledger, approved models, and accepted body section packets. Draft this section last even though it is assembled first. Summarize context, framework abstraction, evidence posture, and limitations without citations where venue conventions discourage them. Introduce no claim, number, contribution, acronym, or term absent from accepted body packets. If body evidence is incomplete, say the paper is descriptive and avoid novelty, comparative, causal, and efficacy wording. Emit an `abstract`-environment TeX fragment and one schema-valid section sidecar with the blueprint's frozen source `input_digest`, consumed claim IDs, citations, figures/tables, notation, TODOs, warnings, and a local `section_digest`. Never inspect repository files.
