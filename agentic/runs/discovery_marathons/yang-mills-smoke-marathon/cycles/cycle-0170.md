# Reflection-Positivity Closed-Cone Topology

## Candidate Lemma

Collar-to-trace OS positivity bridge: let \(\mathcal A_+\) be the locked gauge-invariant positive-time observable algebra and let \(\mathcal A_+^{\delta}\subset \mathcal A_+\) denote observables whose Euclidean time support is at distance at least \(\delta>0\) from the reflection plane. If regulated Schwinger forms \(S_{a,L}\) are reflection-positive on the corresponding regulated algebra, converge on all collar-separated reflected quadratic forms, and satisfy a uniform positivity-compatible collar approximation estimate as \(\delta\downarrow0\), then the continuum Schwinger functional \(S\) is OS-positive on all of \(\mathcal A_+\).

## Definitions

- source object: a net \(S_{a,L}\) of regulated gauge-invariant Schwinger functionals on a fixed abstract positive-time algebra \(\mathcal A_+\), with embeddings \(J_{a,L}:\mathcal A_+\to\mathcal A_{a,L,+}\), reflection \(\Theta\), and finite OS quadratic forms \(Q_{a,L}(F,c)\).
- target object: a continuum Schwinger functional \(S\) on the same locked gauge-invariant algebra whose reflected finite quadratic forms \(Q_S(F,c)=\sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j S((\Theta F_i)F_j)\) are nonnegative, hence valid input for OS Hilbert-space reconstruction.
- controlled interface: regulated reflection-positive Schwinger forms -> OS-positive continuum Schwinger functions, controlled not merely by weak distributional convergence but by convergence of the reflected quadratic probes used in OS positivity.
- same-theory condition: the abstract observables \(F\in\mathcal A_+\) are identified before and after cutoff removal by product-, involution-, reflection-, and gauge-invariant embeddings \(J_{a,L}\); no gauge fixing, quotient replacement, or change from Wilson/composite gauge-invariant probes to a different test algebra is allowed.
- \(\mathcal A_+^{\delta}\) is the collar subalgebra generated by smeared Wilson loops, smeared curvature polynomials, or other gauge-invariant probes whose supports lie in \(t\ge\delta\).
- A positivity-compatible collar approximation is a family \(R_\delta:\mathcal A_+\to\mathcal A_+^{\delta}\) such that \(R_\delta F\to F\) in the observable topology and the reflected quadratic error is uniformly small at the regulated and continuum levels.

## Assumptions

- For every cutoff pair \((a,L)\), \(Q_{a,L}(F,c)\ge0\) for all finite \(F_i\in\mathcal A_+\) after applying the fixed embedding \(J_{a,L}\).
- For every \(\delta>0\), every finite \(F_i\in\mathcal A_+^{\delta}\), and every coefficient vector \(c\), the limit \(\lim_{a\downarrow0,L\uparrow\infty}Q_{a,L}(F,c)=Q_S(F,c)\) exists.
- There are seminorms \(\rho_m\) on \(\mathcal A_+\) and a modulus \(\omega_F(\delta)\to0\) such that the cutoff reflected quadratic errors between \(F_i\) and \(R_\delta F_i\) are bounded uniformly in \((a,L)\) after the renormalized observables are embedded.
- The convergence topology is strong enough to pass mixed reflected forms \(S_{a,L}((\Theta R_\delta F_i)F_j)\), not only unmixed Euclidean moments.
- Reflection preserves the regulator and the embeddings commute with reflection on the nose or up to an error already absorbed by \(\omega_F(\delta)\).

## Equations

- regulated OS form on locked algebra: `Q_{a,L}(F,c)=\sum_{i,j}\overline{c_i}c_j\,S_{a,L}\big((\Theta J_{a,L}F_i)\,J_{a,L}F_j\big)\ge0`. Valid when: Valid for every finite positive-time gauge-invariant family if the regulator action and measure are reflection-positive and the embeddings do not change the observable algebra.
- collar convergence: `F_i\in\mathcal A_+^{\delta}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Q_{a,L}(F,c)\to Q_S(F,c)`. Valid when: Valid when the RG cutoff-removal topology dominates all reflected finite quadratic forms with a positive distance from the reflection plane.
- uniform collar trace estimate: `\left|Q_{a,L}(F,c)-Q_{a,L}(R_\delta F,c)\right|\le C(F,c)\,\omega_F(\delta),\qquad \omega_F(\delta)\downarrow0`. Valid when: Valid only if the multiscale bounds control reflection-plane approach, including contact terms of renormalized gauge-invariant composite observables.
- closed positivity passage: `Q_S(F,c)=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}\,\lim_{a,L}Q_{a,L}(R_\delta F,c)\ge0`. Valid when: Valid after the two limits are justified by collar convergence plus the uniform trace estimate; otherwise this equation is exactly where a falsifier can enter.

## Attempted Derivation

- Fix a finite family \(F_1,\dots,F_n\in\mathcal A_+\) and coefficients \(c\). Choose collar approximants \(F_i^{\delta}=R_\delta F_i\in\mathcal A_+^{\delta}\).
- For each \(\delta>0\), regulated reflection positivity gives \(Q_{a,L}(F^{\delta},c)\ge0\). Collar convergence then yields \(Q_S(F^{\delta},c)=\lim_{a,L}Q_{a,L}(F^{\delta},c)\ge0\). This part is formal and valid once the RG topology controls separated reflected probes.
- Use the cutoff-uniform estimate \(|Q_{a,L}(F,c)-Q_{a,L}(F^{\delta},c)|\le C(F,c)\omega_F(\delta)\) and pass to the limit in \((a,L)\) on both terms. This would imply \(|Q_S(F,c)-Q_S(F^{\delta},c)|\le C(F,c)\omega_F(\delta)\).
- Let \(\delta\downarrow0\). Since each \(Q_S(F^{\delta},c)\ge0\) and the difference tends to zero, conclude \(Q_S(F,c)\ge0\). Thus the OS cone is reached for the full positive-time algebra rather than only for observables separated from the reflection plane.
- The derivation also shows necessity in practice: if an RG topology fails to make the maps \(S\mapsto Q_S(F,c)\) continuous or lower-semicontinuous, the closed-cone step has no valid input even though every cutoff theory is positive.

Blocking gap: The missing analytic estimate is the positivity-compatible trace/collar bound for nonlocal gauge-invariant observables and renormalized local composites as their support approaches the reflection plane. In Yang-Mills this is not a harmless density argument because reflected products can create contact terms at \(t=0\), perimeter/cusp divergences for Wilson loops, and renormalization mixing for local gauge-invariant composites.

## Falsifiers

- A concrete falsifier would be a sequence of gauge-invariant Wilson-loop observables whose loops approach or touch the reflection plane while the reflected quadratic error has a cutoff-dependent cusp or perimeter counterterm with no \(\delta\to0\) modulus.
- Another falsifier is RG convergence that is valid for separated Euclidean correlations but fails for mixed reflected products \((\Theta F_i)F_j\), so the topology is too weak to identify \(Q_S(F,c)\) as a limit of \(Q_{a,L}(F,c)\).
- A same-theory falsifier occurs if positivity is proved only after replacing the locked gauge-invariant algebra by a gauge-fixed or quotient algebra that is not known to reconstruct the intended Yang-Mills observables.
- Reflection positivity might be valid for the lattice transfer matrix but not for the renormalized continuum Schwinger functions if counterterms needed for cutoff removal are not reflection-even or do not commute with the embeddings.
- A trace-extension falsifier occurs if collar positivity holds for every \(\delta>0\) but the limiting quadratic form at \(\delta=0\) acquires an uncontrolled negative contact term.

## Learning

Cycle 170 identifies the next non-tautological lemma: OS positivity should be proved first on a collar algebra away from the reflection plane and then extended by a uniform trace/collar estimate. This separates three layers: the closed cone is valid in \(\tau_{OS}\), RG convergence must dominate collar-separated reflected probes, and Yang-Mills-specific analysis must control reflection-plane contact, cusp, and composite-renormalization terms. The word 'same theory' has become a mathematical condition on embeddings, not a slogan.
