# Central Formal Conceptual Routes

## Purpose

Use Category theory and HoTT as languages for high-level conceptual invention,
without letting formal elegance displace the physics question. This skill gives
formal abstraction a central but nonexclusive place inside a theory-driven
discovery cycle. Its comparison set is exactly:

1. categorical;
2. HoTT;
3. Category then HoTT;
4. HoTT then Category; and
5. capable nonformal reasoning.

The nonformal route is a real competitor. It may win or co-win. Never demote it
merely because it lacks formal vocabulary, and never reward a formal route for
using Category/HoTT terminology.

## Reuse Before Creating Anything New

Reuse these existing capabilities rather than duplicating them:

- `reflective-conceptual-discovery` and `reflective-theory-synthesizer` for the
  creative/philosophical core and its iterative mutation;
- `frontier-literature-assimilator`, the raw-capture/semantic-snapshot bridge,
  and conceptual-ancestry v3 for current literature as a causal discovery
  input;
- `high-risk-hypothesis-forger` and the parallel portfolio for aggressive
  intuitive hypotheses and conceptual-corner coverage;
- `categorical-concept-forger` and
  `hott-equivalence-coherence-forger` for lens-specific heuristics;
- `skills/common/formal_concept_transformations.py` for the seven finite
  constructive transformation kinds;
- `skills/common/formal_concept_sequence_algebra.py` for exact ordered
  Category/HoTT composition and critical pairs;
- `ResearchWorkerAdapter` for supervised process attempts, equal budgets,
  immutable receipts, and terminal evidence;
- `ym-categorical-hott-physics-bridge` only for a Yang--Mills-specific
  handback after the generic route packet exists; and
- `formal-claim-producer` only after a later theory commit selects a typed
  formalization seed.

Do not invoke the historical four-route `central_formal_routes` prototype as
current authority. It is history-only evidence because it accepts caller plans,
self-mints a commit flag, folds common-ancestor deltas, merges the two sequence
orders, and structurally quarantines its nonformal control.

## Required Preconditions

Require all of the following before route authorship:

- one active-current literature snapshot, its retained raw capture, and a
  validated non-conflating bridge between the two identities;
- exactly one conceptual-ancestry v3 artifact for the candidate theory core;
- a deeply reflected conceptual core with explicit target, physical carrier,
  regime, quantifiers, protected observables/successes, open burdens, and a
  correction/falsifier;
- one carrier-specific decision rule frozen before routes, stating an
  incumbent research action and the alternative action that an admissible
  conceptual handback would trigger;
- one method-neutral seven-criterion Pareto adjudication rule frozen before
  routes; and
- a real current `VerifiedCommit` containing the exact canonical plan-free
  input artifact.

The committed input must not contain route plans, witnesses chosen for a route,
route scores, winners, proof results, or same-cycle experimental authority.
Reject a detached mapping, a non-head commit, a noncanonical artifact, a stale
literature basis, or a decision rule that changes the target, carrier, regime,
quantifiers, or burden identity.

## Creative Core Before Formalization

Keep reflective creativity central. Before freezing the plan-free input:

- surface the deepest unresolved conceptual tension rather than optimizing an
  easy formal subproblem;
- produce aggressive high-risk/high-reward hypotheses across genuinely
  different conceptual corners;
- ask what ontology, explanatory dependence, preserved distinction, or notion
  of identity must change for the target to become intelligible;
- run philosophical counter-readings that challenge the framing, the
  explanatory primitive, and the assumed direction of dependence;
- seek unification only when the proposal preserves target-relevant
  differences and creates a sharper correction or falsifier; and
- retain plural hypotheses when evidence does not justify convergence.

Formal routes transform this committed creative core. They do not author its
physics target or retroactively redefine relevance.

## Five Matched Postcommit Authors

Call `agentic.discovery.central_conceptual_routes_v3` with a live transaction
store, the store-reopened current `VerifiedCommit`, the exact committed input
path, and an output root.

The orchestrator must:

- reopen the artifact from the verified commit under one store-owned context;
- compare every portable commit identity field with the supplied capability;
- preserve and distribute identical committed input bytes and content digest;
- start five distinct registered worker processes with identical budgets and
  opportunity;
- derive plans only inside each worker after the commit boundary;
- make both ordered routes execute actual sequential transformation replay;
- terminalize every route and join only after all five terminal receipts;
- record whether all PIDs are distinct and their execution intervals overlap;
  and
- keep cognitive, semantic, hidden-input, and external independence null even
  when process overlap is observed.

An overlap failure blocks matched-comparison claims. A process failure remains
visible and cannot be replaced by a late or caller-authored output.

## Plan Authorship and Sequence Discipline

Each formal worker must enumerate an exact finite witness from the committed
core. It may return typed `unavailable`; it may not ask the caller to fill in a
witness or invent an inhabitant to make a route succeed.

For sequential routes:

- Category→HoTT means apply the categorical plan, validate the exact
  intermediate core, then reapply the HoTT plan to that core;
- HoTT→Category means the reverse exact sequence;
- retain productive noncommutation as plural candidates;
- treat an isomorphism-search ceiling as unavailable, not as inequality;
- quarantine only the ordering that erases a protected observable or depends
  on free completion; and
- if only one order completes, retain its finite candidate while keeping
  precommitted decision replay ineligible until the sequence contract permits
  it.

Never replace these rules with unioning two deltas constructed independently
from their common ancestor.

## Shared Physics Handback

All five routes, formal and nonformal, must pass through the same handback
kernel. Require:

- exact target, obligation, physical carrier, regime, and quantifiers;
- the full protected-success set;
- one explicit changed conceptual resource or explanatory dependency;
- localized changed burdens;
- a concrete correction and discriminating falsifier;
- a carrier-specific next theoretical action;
- every untouched physics gate; and
- replay of the precommitted incumbent/eligible research-action rule.

The replay may change a theory-research action. It does not establish that the
candidate is physically true, valuable, deep, unified, novel, a theorem, a
continuum construction, or a Yang--Mills solution. Preserve those nulls in
every route, handback, judge record, and final packet.

## Blind Set-Valued Adjudication

Send only opaque candidate IDs and shared content projections to the separate
judge process. Exclude route ID, producer ID, lens labels, formal status, and
caller rankings. Do not award a method bonus.

Compute the precommitted criteria exactly:

1. target relevance;
2. protected-success preservation;
3. consequence specificity;
4. burden localization;
5. unification compression;
6. discriminating falsifier; and
7. conceptual depth.

Return the complete Pareto-nondominated set, including ties. Reattach route
identities only after the judge has sealed its content-only result. The result
is an incubating preference set for later reflective theory synthesis, not an
automatic theory promotion.

## Theory-First Return and Secondary Experiments

Return the plural preference set to a distinct N+1 reflective cycle. That cycle
may synthesize, compare, demote, or preserve candidates before any active theory
commit. Formal-claim production occurs only from a later committed typed seed.

Use symbolic reasoning or simulation only when the theory supplies a mature,
discriminating question that separates live conceptual alternatives. An
experiment may test a finite witness, expose a protected-observable collapse,
or falsify a carrier-specific consequence. It may not select the theory in the
same cycle, substitute numerical fit for understanding, or become the main
discovery driver.

## Failure and Quarantine

Fail closed on:

- stale or substituted literature, ancestry, commit, registry, code, schema,
  or artifact bytes;
- caller-authored plans, scores, or winners;
- missing, duplicate, reused, or nonterminal process receipts;
- unequal author budgets or nonidentical committed input bytes;
- a sequence that skips intermediate replay;
- protected-observable erasure or free-completion assumption-away;
- route labels in the judge projection;
- structural exclusion of the capable nonformal route;
- a decision replay not fixed before authorship; or
- any escalation of creativity, depth, merit, physics, truth, theorem,
  continuum, novelty, external-validation, or Yang--Mills authority.

## Output

Produce one packet conforming to
`agentic/spec/central_conceptual_route_packet_v3.schema.json`, with:

- the exact verified-commit and committed-input descriptor;
- five ordered route terminals and receipt digests;
- a five-party terminal/overlap barrier;
- every accepted, quarantined, unavailable, and failed route outcome;
- shared carrier-specific handbacks;
- the separate blind-judge receipt and content-only preference set; and
- explicit theory-first, experiment-secondary, and epistemic null boundaries.

Hand the packet to later reflective synthesis. Do not publish it as a discovery
result merely because the local protocol completed.
