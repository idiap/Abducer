# Yang--Mills Categorical/HoTT Physics Bridge

This is a theory-first relevance bridge, not a proof engine. It uses category theory and HoTT as high-level languages for conceptual invention while forcing every abstraction back onto exact Yang--Mills carriers, quantifiers, burdens, and correction routes. The bridge packet itself has no physics-value or investment authority.

## Typed Structure Semantics

Emit exactly fourteen rows in this order: `four_dimensional_pure_yang_mills`, `compact_simple_gauge_group_scope`, `gauge_invariance`, `observable_algebra`, `regulator_ledger`, `volume_ledger`, `coupling_trajectory`, `physical_normalization`, `locality`, `reflection_positivity`, `theory_identity`, `continuum_limit_order`, `os_reconstruction_burden`, and `positive_hamiltonian_gap_target`.

Use four kinds without conflation:

1. A `fixed_target_commitment` has status `required`. Required means frozen as part of the question, never proved by the bridge.
2. A `transport_invariant` is a proof burden, including `theory_identity`. Under the active absence-only continuum-authority contract it may be `conditional`, `open`, or `failed` at the finite-bridge level; serialized `proved` promotion is rejected for every transport row.
3. The `os_reconstruction_burden` is an `open_reconstruction_burden`; it is not a preserved invariant.
4. The `positive_hamiltonian_gap_target` is a `desired_consequence`; it is not a premise or preserved invariant.

The proof-status lattice is `failed < open < conditional < proved`; required commitments sit outside that proof lattice. The v6 schema retains `proved` only as an explicitly rejected future authority state. No approved continuum checker is registered, so the serialized continuum authority is an exact unavailable projection and finite evidence can reach at most `conditional`. Declarations alone can establish `required`, but can never establish `conditional` or `proved`.

Categorical equivalence or HoTT identity/equivalence is never physical equivalence. No finite bridge row proves continuum existence, Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction, or a Hamiltonian mass gap.

## Mathematical Invariants

Preserve these invariants as conjunctions, never as an average score or an elegance judgment:

1. **Target identity:** source and target remain four-dimensional pure Yang--Mills for every declared compact simple gauge group. A representation change may alter `K`, `D`, `G`, `L`, or `Q`, but it may not change dimension, purity, gauge-group scope, physical normalization, or the state-separating observable target.

3. **Finite HoTT laws:** each map and inverse acts totally on declared terms; each term-level path has exactly one indexed level-one fiber with identical ambient type and boundaries; inverse homotopies and transports replay; and coherence or noncoherence is decided only on the sealed finite path space and truncation policy.
4. **Quantifier and evidence closure:** every one of the fourteen structure rows binds its canonical target fields, quantifier subset, derived evidence, obligation, carrier, and structure-specific correction tests. Declarations establish only `required`; finite forge, witness, and calibration evidence cannot demonstrate continuum quantifiers.
5. **Status ceiling:** under v1 unavailable continuum authority, transport rows can be at most `conditional`, while OS reconstruction and the positive Hamiltonian-gap target remain `open` or `failed`. A finite obstruction can defeat this bridge route without deciding the continuum theory.
6. **Execution identity:** every accepted category/HoTT lens is backed by a v2 formal-lens execution receipt whose forge packet v5, parallel evaluation v4, dependency-authority v2, executable digests, current-literature projection, meaning compilation, isomorphism-normalized model identity, witness, lens-native consequence, and physics handback are transitively replayed. The receipt is finite/internal only and cannot attest production use or cognitive independence.
7. **Physics handback completeness:** the returned burden matrix contains exactly fourteen canonically ordered rows and binds the same target, quantifiers, evidence, carriers, transfer ledger, burdens, corrections, complete typed unavailable continuum-authority object, and failure witness as the bridge packet. The upstream and bridge route decisions must name the declared incumbent and differ substantively by decision kind, normalized action, or target burden. Missing, inapplicable, non-physics, or unchanged decisions force `quarantine_non_investment`.
8. **Value null:** `authority_distinct_physics_value_evidence_status` is `unavailable`, its evidence-ID list is empty, and `physics_value_authorized` and `investment_authorized` remain false. Formal coherence, internal repeatability, or a positive local ablation can justify only `refine_bridge`; they cannot establish physical equivalence, general conceptual value, continuum existence, OS reconstruction, a spectral theorem, or a mass gap.
