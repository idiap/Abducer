# Prove Rigidity Transfer

## Purpose

Build the bridge from an independently verified regulated estimate to exclusion of continuum spectrum in `(0,m)`. The bridge is a theorem only after positivity, convergence, observable completeness, and uniform physical normalization are supplied.

## Proof Pipeline

1. Freeze the source predicate, all cutoff and volume quantifiers, and the physical decay rate.
2. Identify the positive Euclidean semigroup or transfer operator. Prove the relevant reflection-positivity statement rather than importing it informally.
3. For each centered physical vector `psi`, represent its correlation as a positive spectral measure:
   `C_psi(t)=<psi,e^{-tH}psi>=int exp(-t lambda) dmu_psi(lambda)`.
4. Use a uniform estimate `C_psi(t)<=K_psi exp(-m t)` for all physical `t>=0` to prove `mu_psi((0,m))=0`.
5. Prove that vectors generated by the approved observable class are dense in the vacuum-orthogonal physical Hilbert space. Otherwise report only a sectorial gap.
6. Transfer approximate estimates with an explicit error term and topology. Show that the error vanishes before taking the large-time conclusion.
7. Compose representation morphisms only after checking their certificates and accumulated error budget.
8. Prove existence of a finite-energy nonvacuum vector when the target definition requires spectrum above the gap to be nonempty.

## Core Lemma

For a positive self-adjoint `H`, if every vector in a dense subspace of the vacuum complement satisfies

`<psi,e^{-tH}psi> <= K_psi e^{-m t}` for every `t>=0`,

then `spec(H)` on that complement is contained in `[m,infinity)`. Prove this by positivity of spectral measures; do not infer it from oscillatory real-time estimates.

## Required Audits

- Reflection positivity survives every limiting operation used.
- The decay rate is in physical units and uniform in volume and cutoff.
- Contact terms and smearing conventions do not invalidate the estimate.
- Observable convergence is strong enough to pass the quadratic forms.
- The vacuum is unique if uniqueness is part of the claimed gap statement.
- No hidden orthogonal sector evades the observable class.

## Counterexamples to Run

Test finite-volume fake gaps, a direct-sum hidden gapless sector, nonuniform cutoff gaps, signed spectral measures, and errors that vanish only for fixed time but not on the time scale used to extract the gap.
