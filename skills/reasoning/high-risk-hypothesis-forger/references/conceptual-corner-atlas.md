# Conceptual corner atlas

Use these families to force structural diversity, not as a vocabulary checklist.

| Corner | Provocation | Required audit |
|---|---|---|
| Ontology inversion | What if the presumed object is really a relation, process, constraint, absence, or equivalence class? | Recover stable identity and incumbent successes. |
| Missing mediator | What entity, carrier, interface, witness, or latent variable would make the opposition expressible? | Show it is not defined by the desired conclusion. |
| Observer–realizer split | Does what can be accessed, verified, or represented differ from what exists or can be constructed? | Separate epistemic access from ontology. |
| Scale/regime reversal | Does explanation run from local to global, global to local, UV to IR, finite to asymptotic, or the reverse? | Preserve limit order and transfer scope. |
| Failure as structure | Could repeated obstruction be an invariant, conservation law, curvature, defect, or no-go principle? | Avoid rebranding ignorance as mechanism. |
| Duality/completion | Is the missing theory a dual, completion, quotient, lift, adjunction, or change of equivalence policy? | Record lost information and nontrivial automorphisms. |
| Emergence/stabilization | Could stable objects or laws emerge as fixed points, attractors, descent data, or coherent processes? | Prove non-vacuity and recovery of theorem subjects. |
| Cross-field transfer | Does a mechanism from another field play the same structural role? | Inventory preserved and broken invariants before analogy. |
| Counter-consensus | Which taboo assumption, standard decomposition, or accepted direction might be contingent? | State the protected success being risked. |
| Unification conjecture | Are several barriers shadows of one deeper mechanism? | Preserve decisive differences and boundary cases. |
| Negative theory | Is the right advance an impossibility, obstruction taxonomy, or theorem about why routes fail? | Make the no-go scope precise and non-circular. |
| Reflexive inversion | Is the workflow, representation, or proof norm manufacturing the apparent problem? | Produce a changed mathematical object or question. |

For each chosen corner, state the new affordance it creates and the fastest way
it could collapse into a proxy, tautology, or existing idea.
