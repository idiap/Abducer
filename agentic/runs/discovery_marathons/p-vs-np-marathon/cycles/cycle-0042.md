# Cycle 42: distributional cap certificate for one-sided semantic separators

## Candidate Lemma

For the high-q polylog-query generated-OR carrier, a valid source lower bound can be certified by a distributional cap on every small one-sided local SAT predicate. This converts the Cycle 41 term-cover versus semantic-separator gap into a quantitative test: low cap implies Sep_N^{1s}(E) is superthreshold; a large cap is a concrete falsifier for the source lower bound, while the target bridge remains unchanged.

## Definitions

- source object: H_N(x)=OR_{j<=Q} SAT(F_{N,j}(x)) on N-bit inputs, with Q=N^q, L=(log N)^d, d>1, and all F_{N,j} generated by a circuit G_N of size N^{rho+o(1)}.
- target object: SAT notin P/poly obtained only by contrapositive substitution from polynomial-size SAT circuits into the generated-OR carrier; this is route-level progress, not a proof of P != NP.
- controlled interface: local L-bit predicates P_{j,r} of circuit size at most s, composed as P_{j,r}(F_{N,j}(x)), plus equality patching for E missed positive N-bit inputs at cost O(EN).
- target-preserving condition: max{q,rho}<1+epsilon, q>(1+epsilon)/2, L=(log N)^d with d>1, and no advice leakage other than the charged nonuniform local predicates and patching equalities.
- source lower-bound model: nonuniform fan-in two Boolean circuits for H_N, with the desired source claim C(H_N)>N^{1+epsilon-o(1)}.
- one-sided soundness: P_{j,r}(F_{N,j}(x))=1 is forbidden for every negative bag x with H_N(x)=0; the predicate need only be sound on the actual generated range.
- separator measure: Sep_N^{1s}(E;s) is the minimum K(s+L^{O(1)})+EN over K size-s local predicates that hit all but E positive inputs and have no false positives on negative inputs.
- distributional cap: for a distribution mu_N^+ on H_N^{-1}(1), A_N(s)=max_{j,P} Pr_{x~mu_N^+}[P(F_{N,j}(x))=1], where P ranges over size-s one-sided predicates.

## Assumptions

- epsilon>0 is fixed and independent of N.
- q and rho are constants satisfying q>(1+epsilon)/2 and max{q,rho}<1+epsilon.
- The generator outputs Q formulas of length L with total charged implementation size N^{rho+o(1)}.
- mu_N^+ is supported on positive bags and every exceptional set of at most E positives has mu_N^+-mass at most beta_N(E).
- Local predicate size s is charged nonuniformly; the lemma does not assume uniform construction of the predicates.

## Equations

- carrier_definition: `H_N(x)=OR_{j<=N^q} SAT(F_{N,j}(x)), |F_{N,j}(x)|=L=(log N)^d`. Valid when: Valid when the carrier is the generated-OR source problem with fixed high-q parameters and a named SAT encoding.
- target_substitution_bridge: `SAT in SIZE(n^c) => C(H_N) <= N^{rho+o(1)} + N^q (log N)^{dc+O(1)} = N^{max{rho,q}+o(1)}`. Valid when: Applies after the reduction preserves the generator size, query length, and nonuniform circuit-size accounting.
- cap_profile: `A_N(s)=max_{j,P one-sided, size(P)<=s} Pr_{x~mu_N^+}[P(F_{N,j}(x))=1]`. Valid when: Valid when one-sided soundness is imposed on the actual generated range, not on an informal global SAT promise.
- union_cover_lower_bound: `K A_N(s) >= 1 - beta_N(E)`. Valid when: Valid when K local predicates miss at most E positive inputs and mu_N^+ gives those exceptions total mass beta_N(E).
- separator_lower_bound: `Sep_N^{1s}(E;s) >= (1-beta_N(E))(s+L^{O(1)})/A_N(s) + EN`. Valid when: Requires the cap bound for every allowed local predicate size and the standard equality patching charge for missed positives.
- route_threshold: `A_N(s) <= (1-beta_N(E))(s+L^{O(1)})/N^{1+epsilon-o(1)} => Sep_N^{1s}(E;s) >= N^{1+epsilon-o(1)}`. Valid when: Applies in the source lower-bound regime where separator cost is the only remaining known nonuniform leakage channel.

## Attempted Derivation

- Instantiate F_{N,j}(x) as polylog-length k-CNF formulas with v=beta log N variables and density near the satisfiability threshold; set Q=N^q with q in ((1+epsilon)/2,1+epsilon).
- Try to upper-bound A_N(s) by first bounding hit probability of assignment or partial-term predicates under mu_N^+.
- Attempt a union bound over all size-s L-input circuits P, using the hope that any large accepted set contains generated unsatisfiable formulas and hence violates one-sided soundness.
- The count of circuits is 2^{O(s log s)}, and for s=N^alpha this is far too large for term-cover estimates over individual witnesses.
- More seriously, one-sided soundness is semantic on the generated range: a circuit may recognize a correlated satisfiable slice with no small witness-term description.

Blocking gap: Term-cover randomness does not imply a cap bound for arbitrary one-sided semantic predicates. The missing theorem is a one-sided conditional pseudorandomness statement for the distribution of generated formulas inside positive bags.

## Falsifiers

- semantic planted-slice falsifier: a small predicate recognizes a satisfiable subset hit by most positive bags while avoiding all negative bags.
- large cap-profile falsifier: for some s, A_N(s) is much larger than s/N^{1+epsilon}, yielding a subthreshold separator.
- exception concentration falsifier: the chosen positive distribution assigns large mass to E patchable positives, making beta_N(E) too large for the cover lower bound.
- nonuniform leakage falsifier: the generator range at one position admits a small promise SAT labeler even though global SAT on length L remains hard by exhaustive lookup.
- threshold-collapse falsifier: q>=1+epsilon or rho>=1+epsilon destroys the target-preserving substitution bridge.

## Learning

Cycle 42 turns the one-sided semantic-separator obstruction into an exact distributional cap profile A_N(s). The valid source route now needs a superthreshold cap-to-separator lower bound, not merely random witness or term-cover entropy. The target bridge is preserved, but any large one-sided cap is a concrete falsifier for the generated-OR lower bound.

# Barrier-Aware Lower-Bound Route: polynomial-degree target gate

## Candidate Lemma

A polynomial-degree lower bound transfers to circuit lower bounds only through a target-degree upper envelope. For unrestricted SIZE(m^k), the envelope is already the maximum possible value m, witnessed by easy P functions, so any degree-only route to P != NP has a quantitative threshold collapse.

## Definitions

- source object: a lower bound on a Boolean-function representation measure mu, with mu in {exact multilinear degree over F, sign degree over R, bounded-error approximate degree over R}, evaluated on SAT_m or a polynomially padded SAT slice.
- target object: nonuniform unrestricted Boolean circuits SIZE(m^k) on the same m input bits, with fan-in two AND/OR/NOT gates and constants.
- controlled interface: a target-envelope compiler B_{mu,T}(m,k) proving mu(C) <= B_{mu,T}(m,k) for every target circuit C, without changing the slice or adding advice.
- target-preserving condition: the source lower bound is useful only if mu(SAT_m) > B_{mu,T}(m,k); for P vs NP this must hold for T = SIZE(m^k) for every fixed k.

## Assumptions

- SAT_m is totalized on all m-bit encodings, with malformed encodings rejected.
- The lower-bound proof uses only the numerical measure mu, not SAT-specific nonblack-box syntax.
- The target class is unrestricted nonuniform Boolean circuits, not AC0, formulas, branching programs, or algebraic circuits.
- k >= 1, so SIZE(m^k) contains linear-size AND and parity implementations.

## Equations

- target_envelope: `B_{\mu,T}(m,k)=\max\{\mu(g): g \in T_{m,k}\}`. Valid when: Valid when the target class and input length are fixed before maximizing over all target functions.
- sound_transfer: `\mu(SAT_m)>B_{\mu,T}(m,k) \Rightarrow SAT_m \notin T_{m,k}`. Valid when: Applies after the compiler preserves the exact slice, uniformity commitments, and threshold parameters.
- unrestricted_collapse: `B_{\mu,SIZE}(m,k)=m`. Valid when: Valid when T is unrestricted Boolean SIZE(m^k), k >= 1, and mu is exact degree, sign degree, or bounded-error approximate degree.
- maximum_degree_ceiling: `\mu(SAT_m) \le m`. Valid when: Valid when SAT_m is treated as a total Boolean function on m encoded input bits.

## Attempted Derivation

- Define the route measure mu and the target envelope B_{mu,T}(m,k).
- The transfer implication is immediate: if SAT_m had a T-circuit C, then mu(SAT_m)=mu(C)<=B_{mu,T}(m,k), contradicting the source lower bound.
- For every total m-bit Boolean function, exact degree, sign degree, and bounded-error approximate degree are at most m by exact multilinear representation.
- The upper bound is tight inside unrestricted polynomial-size circuits: AND_m has exact degree m over every field, and PARITY_m has sign degree and approximate degree m while having O(m)-size Boolean circuits.
- Therefore B_{mu,SIZE}(m,k)=m for k>=1, so a degree-only proof would need mu(SAT_m)>m, impossible for a total m-bit function.
- For padded SAT with m=n^beta, the measured degree is at most the core length n=m^{1/beta} when the padding bits are ignored, so padding worsens the threshold rather than preserving it.

Blocking gap: The missing bridge is a nontrivial compiler from arbitrary polynomial-size Boolean circuits to representations of degree below m. Easy max-degree P functions falsify such a compiler for the standard degree measures.

## Falsifiers

- AND_m is in linear size and has exact degree m over every field, a falsifier to any exact-degree envelope B<m for unrestricted circuits.
- PARITY_m is in polynomial size and has sign degree and approximate degree m, a falsifier to sign-degree or approximate-degree transfer to unrestricted SIZE.
- PaddedSAT_{n^beta} ignores padding and has degree at most n=m^{1/beta}, a falsifier to padding-amplifies-degree intuition.
- For exact F2 degree, the property deg(f)>D with D<m is large and constructive, so usefulness against PRF-capable targets triggers the natural-proof falsifier rather than escaping it.

## Learning

Cycle 42 certifies a concrete threshold collapse for polynomial-degree lower-bound routes. The valid bridge is mu(SAT_m)>B_{mu,T}(m,k); for unrestricted SIZE(m^k), B=m is tight because easy P functions attain maximum degree. Thus degree-only lower bounds may be useful for restricted classes with low-degree compilers, but they do not constitute P-vs-NP progress.

# MCSP / Meta-Complexity Carrier Transfer

## Candidate Lemma

Low-ANF-degree global invariants cannot be universal MCSP no-side certificates. If a nonempty acceptance predicate A_m on N=2^m truth-table bits has Boolean ANF degree K_m, then it accepts some table of Hamming weight at most K_m, hence a table with unrestricted circuit size O(K_m m). Thus any universal source claiming MCSP threshold 2^{epsilon m} is invalid when K_m m^{O(1)}<2^{epsilon m}. For algebraic fibers p_i(x)=b_i of degree at most d, K_m<=dT, so low-degree nonlinear syndromes fail just as affine syndromes did.

## Definitions

- source object: a uniform Boolean acceptance predicate A_m on N=2^m truth-table bits, or a fiber p_i(x)=b_i, whose accepted tables are claimed MCSP no-side rows.
- target object: an MCSP no-side carrier at threshold s(m)=2^{epsilon m} feeding the existing E lower-bound extraction with losses kappa and distinct-image entropy D.
- controlled interface: E-uniform canonical enumeration of accepted images, no nonuniform length selector, and unrestricted circuit size used for MCSP soundness.
- target-preserving condition: every table accepted as a universal hard row must have unrestricted circuit complexity greater than 2^{epsilon m}, and multiplexed existential variants must name the hard-row selector or image-rank recovery.
- ANF degree K_m is the degree of A_m in F_2[x_1,...,x_N]/(x_i^2-x_i).
- A t-sparse truth table has unrestricted circuit size O(tm) by hardwiring its support and OR-ing equality tests.

## Assumptions

- A_m is not identically zero on the Boolean cube.
- The source uses universal soundness for accepted rows; existential hard-row variants require an independent selector or multiplexing certificate.
- Circuit size is unrestricted nonuniform Boolean circuit size on m input bits.
- The MCSP threshold is s(m)=2^{epsilon m} for a fixed epsilon>0.
- For the fiber corollary, A_b(x)=1 exactly when all equations p_i(x)=b_i hold over F_2.

## Equations

- acceptance-indicator-degree: `A_m(x)=\sum_{S\subseteq[N],\ |S|\le K_m} a_S\prod_{j\in S}x_j,\qquad K_m=\deg_{ANF}(A_m).`. Valid when: Valid when acceptance is viewed as a Boolean function on the full truth table and the ANF is reduced modulo x_j^2=x_j.
- low-support-contamination: `\min\{|y|_1:A_m(y)=1\}\le K_m.`. Valid when: Valid when A_m is nonzero; choose a maximum-degree monomial and use Mobius inversion on its subcube.
- fiber-degree-bound: `A_b(x)=\prod_{i=1}^{T}(1+p_i(x)+b_i),\qquad \deg(A_b)\le \sum_i\deg(p_i)\le dT.`. Valid when: Valid when the source invariant is an exact conjunction of equations p_i(x)=b_i over F_2 on Boolean truth-table variables.
- easy-contaminant-circuit: `C(y)\le O(|y|_1\,m)\le O(K_m m).`. Valid when: Applies when unrestricted circuits may hardwire the support of y and test equality against each m-bit input.
- soundness-threshold-failure: `K_m\le 2^{\epsilon m}/m^{c}\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists y\,[A_m(y)=1\ \wedge\ C(y)<2^{\epsilon m}].`. Valid when: Requires c large enough to dominate equality-test overhead and the MCSP threshold is s(m)=2^{epsilon m}.

## Attempted Derivation

- Represent the claimed universal invariant A_m as its reduced ANF over the N=2^m truth-table coordinates.
- Let S be a maximum-size monomial with coefficient a_S=1, so |S|=K_m.
- Use Mobius inversion on the subcube supported by S: a_S=\oplus_{U\subseteq S} A_m(1_U).
- Since a_S=1, at least one U subset S has A_m(1_U)=1.
- The accepted table 1_U has support at most K_m and therefore unrestricted circuit size O(K_m m).
- If K_m m^{O(1)}<2^{epsilon m}, this accepted table is below the MCSP no-side threshold, contradicting universal soundness.
- For a conjunction of T equations of degree at most d, the exact acceptance indicator is the product of the equation indicators and has ANF degree at most dT.

Blocking gap: The derivation is a complete no-go for low-ANF-degree universal predicates, but it does not construct the needed high-degree non-natural invariant and it does not eliminate existential hard-row sources with an independent selector.

## Falsifiers

- Low-ANF-degree acceptance K_m<2^{epsilon m}/poly(m) is a concrete falsifier for universal MCSP no-side soundness.
- Quadratic, cubic, or bounded-degree polynomial fibers with dT below the MCSP threshold are contaminated by sparse easy tables.
- Affine syndrome sources remain falsified as the degree-1 special case.
- A high-degree invariant implemented through subthreshold random access is invalid by leakage even if the ANF audit passes.
- A nonuniform length selector or advice-dependent hard-row choice invalidates the target-preserving E extraction.
- Existential hard rows inside a contaminated set do not help unless multiplexing or a uniform hard-row recovery certificate is supplied.

## Learning

Cycle 42 proves a new source-side no-go: any nonempty low-ANF-degree acceptance predicate over truth-table bits accepts a sparse table, so it cannot universally certify MCSP hardness above its ANF degree times equality-test overhead. This extends the affine-syndrome falsifier to bounded-degree nonlinear fibers and identifies high ANF degree, not mere nonlinearity, as the next required invariant.
