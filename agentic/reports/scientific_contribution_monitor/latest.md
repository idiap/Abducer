## Paper Relevance Marker Criteria

- `conceptual_depth`: The result engages a core Yang-Mills bridge obligation rather than a surface analogy: same-theory construction, OS positivity, Wilson observable algebra, boundary OPE, quotient positivity, or spectral reconstruction.
- `conceptual_novelty`: The result contributes a new finite obstruction, bridge lemma, worked Wilson calculation, or transfer boundary rather than repackaging OS or Schur-Feshbach machinery.
- `unblocking_power`: The result decides a concrete next move: prove the finite theorem, compute H,K, certify transfer, backtrack, or quarantine a proxy branch.
- `applicability`: The object can be reused across Wilson families, boundary collars, quotient positivity checks, or reconstruction interfaces under stated assumptions.
- `understanding_gain`: The result explains where the proof pressure lives: finite positivity, same-theory transfer, continuum control, nontriviality, or spectral witness.
- `physical_relevance_and_general_impact`: The result names a physical carrier, target gate, and unification-power payoff: gauge-invariant Wilson/Schwinger observables, OS Hilbert reconstruction, nontriviality, continuum control, or the Hamiltonian spectral-gap interface are compressed by one mechanism rather than listed as adjacent aspirations.

## Stepping-Stone Maturity Levels

- `contribution_shaped`: The branch has an independently meaningful object or obstruction, but it is not yet a publishable stepping stone.
- `near_stepping_stone`: One or two bounded prior-art, disqualifier, proof-shape, or validation tasks could make the candidate paper-note ready.
- `publishable_handoff_ready`: Broad exploration should pause; the candidate should move to validation, prior-art, and manuscript packaging.
