# Source-grounded conceptual inquiry: shared paper contract

This manual authoring contract governs requests to explain or repair the paper's conceptual organization. It replaces operator-first organization in that lane. A taxonomy request may still describe a source's operator classification, but must not silently substitute that task for conceptual inquiry. This document adds no runtime schema, runner stage, or automatic evidence authority.

For an existing-manuscript revision, document the manual evidence basis explicitly:
a frozen source selection with hashes and spans, the reviewed native packet where
needed, a descriptive claim ledger, and a reviewed conceptual model. This is an
alternative authoring route, not a claim that the fixed compiler's closed schemas
were validated. Preserve the same source and claim checks; a free-form summary or
the manuscript itself cannot substitute for underlying conceptual sources.

## Source and organizing question

Start from the actual conceptual situation: the target question, incumbent primitives and relations, representations, explanatory commitments, protected successes, and what remains opaque. Reconstruct the strongest opposed commitments and distinguish contradiction, missing mediation, regime mismatch, underdetermination, and conflicting explanatory ideals. The author must show why this tension matters before introducing an operator vocabulary.

Diagnose the bottleneck with evidence. `conceptual`, `representational`, `explanatory`, and `mixed` diagnoses can motivate conceptual reconstruction. A `not_conceptual` result exits this inquiry to the grounded calculation, formalization, proof, reading, or measurement task; it must not trigger an invented conceptual crisis. An unresolved diagnosis remains unresolved. Conceptual inquiry is the explanatory default for this request, not a claim that all difficult work requires conceptual invention.

The six-lens profile comes from [conceptual frontier deepening, Phase 3](../../../skills/orchestration/conceptual-frontier-deepening/SKILL.md). The broader source prescriptions are [reflective conceptual discovery](../../../skills/orchestration/reflective-conceptual-discovery/SKILL.md), [reflective theory synthesis](../../../skills/reasoning/reflective-theory-synthesizer/SKILL.md), and its [reflection lenses](../../../skills/reasoning/reflective-theory-synthesizer/references/reflection-lenses.md). Resolve them through frozen evidence before attributing behavior. These references ground exposition; reading them is not invoking their runtime or claiming execution.

## What the main text must teach

Carry a concrete situation through reconstruction, diagnosis, materially different alternatives, reflection, comparison, consequences, and revision. This is a revisitable organization of explanatory questions, not a universal stage sequence. Retain the incumbent as an intelligible alternative. Vary primitives, identities, relations, mechanisms, explanatory dependencies, representations, or equivalence policies; renamed candidates and parameter changes alone do not establish conceptual novelty.

For every proposed change explain its trigger, what actually changes, what earlier success and decisive difference it preserves, what it loses, its new risk, and what becomes expressible or understandable. Show a specific before/after result and a boundary case. Preserve target identity, quantifiers, regimes, and nontriviality, or state every deliberate alteration explicitly. Unification by weakening the question or erasing differences fails review.

Explain the six transformations in the conceptual-frontier-deepening Phase 3 profile through concrete questions:

| Lens | Required explanatory content |
| --- | --- |
| Ontology | Which primitives and identity conditions change or remain defensible? |
| Dialectic | What is the strongest internal contradiction or opposed commitment, and what success must each side preserve? |
| Genealogy | Which inherited historical commitment produced the pressure, and what change would remove its accidental constraint? |
| Modality | Which structure is necessary, contingent, gauge-dependent, representational, or relative to a regime? |
| Unification | Which mediator connects cases while preserving their decisive differences? |
| Philosophy of physics | Where relevant, what changes in explanatory direction, observability, emergence, realism, or theory–world linkage? |

These are the named source profile's six transformations, not a universal exhaustive taxonomy or an assertion that every paper case executed them. The broader reflective-conceptual-discovery route selects at least three applicable lenses, including ontology and dialectic for major synthesis, from a richer lens reference. Preserve that variation explicitly. For a nonphysics illustration, label an explanatory interpretation as an editorial adaptation and preserve a `not_applicable` disposition for unsupported physics-specific content; do not pretend it is native physics behavior.

Record each of the six as `applied`, `not_applicable`, or `blocked`, with reason and source. An applied lens records exposed assumption, protected content, changed relation or justified `no_change`, understanding delta, new burden/risk, correction, and discriminating falsifier or unresolved obligation; preserve a physics handback where the source and target require one. A paraphrase is not a transformation. These expository dispositions do not waive requirements when the actual Phase 3 runtime route is invoked, and they do not fabricate its execution receipts.

Compare serious rivals on relevance, contact with the root tension, understanding, explanatory necessity, unification, preservation of difference, depth, generativity, coherence, and truth-aptness. Show trade-offs and plural nondominated possibilities; do not invent a scalar winner, equate list order with preference, or infer authority from an internal review. Explain consequences and new questions, residual tensions, correction conditions, and the next revision or support obligation. Separate conceptual promise, preferred organization, formal support, model support, experiment, and proof. Probes answer a mature theory question when warranted; they are not mandatory prerequisites for conceptual progress.

## Supporting formal language and scope

Native A/D/I and other operator families may annotate one or many inquiry moves, and a move may have several or no justified native annotations. A shared label does not establish temporal order, completeness, implementation equivalence, or a universal exhaustive account of cognition. Preserve the distinction between domain primitives being reconsidered and operators in the framework's vocabulary. Do not start curation from “the eight primitives” or force every explanatory unit into primitive/composition/motif kinds.

Keep conceptual narrative, native declaration, linked procedural prescription, constructed illustration, and observed execution distinct. A conceptual alternative may exceed what a particular native workflow declares; label that relationship, including unresolved producers. Types and category/HoTT notation can clarify commitments after the conceptual issue is explained; matching signatures do not prove semantic preservation or truth.

The existing [step explanation contract](../../framework-paper-workflow-appendix-writer/references/step-explanation-contract.md) remains mandatory for the appendix. Preserve the frozen manifest scope: this paper has 19 workflows and 65 occurrence bindings at the current snapshot. Regeneration must reopen the source inventory, not rely solely on these historical counts. Keep all seven step fields, continuous workflow cases, binding/signature/evidence checks, reviews, and actual manuscript inclusion. A new conceptual center never licenses shrinking that appendix.

## Review and delivery

Review the argument after removing operator symbols: can a reader reconstruct the situation, diagnosis including the exit, opposed systems, actual conceptual changes, preserved differences, plural comparison, and correction conditions? Reject a renamed operator tour, six empty lens labels, conceptual change equated with proof, forced consensus, or unsupported executed stages. Record content-specific findings and revisions, distinguishing semantic review from automated integrity checks.

Use the reviewed conceptual model and claim ledger to synchronize blueprint, body, abstract, introduction, conclusion, captions, figures, and assembly. The paper's first conceptual figure should explain the inquiry and revisitation/exit relations, with arrow meaning stated; move exhaustive operator maps to supporting material. The final gate requires reconciled main-text argument, unchanged complete appendix coverage, updated provenance, actual manuscript integration, fresh compilation, and visual inspection. Do not report CLI support or runtime validation that does not exist.

## Recursive inquiry and workflow relationships

It explains how unresolved conceptual questions produce nested obligations, how returned evidence enables a parent, and how stopping and revisitation relate the full workflow family. Preserve the conceptual-first architecture and independent complete step coverage.
