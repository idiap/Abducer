## Mathematical Invariants

The mathematical objects are retained states `s`, conditional fibers, positive densities `w_s`, measurable regions `G_s` and `B_s`, a next-state map `tau`, a weight `V>=1`, and the weighted partition quantities below. Preserve:

- `0 < Z_G(s)+Z_B(s) < infinity` on every retained state in the declared positive-mass support, and use one measurable disintegration throughout.
- The exact identity `q_V(s)=B_V(s)/((Z_G(s)+Z_B(s))V(s))`; never drop `Z_B`, replace the denominator by an uncontrolled constant, or compare numerator and denominator at different heat times.
- Uniformity of every supremum and constant in the declared finite volumes, boundary laws, cutoffs, and retained states.
- Positivity of `w_s` and `V`, gauge covariance of the good/bad split and `tau`, and preservation of center, orbit/cut-locus, topology, locality, and observable content.
- If `Z_G >= exp(-ell) Z_G_ref` and `B_V <= exp(-F) Z_G_ref V`, then `q_V <= exp(ell-F)` with the same reference normalizer. For a target ceiling `q`, retain the strict invariant `F > ell-log(q)`.
- In a `K+R` decomposition, derive `(K+R)V <= (exp(ell)k+r)V` only from separately proved `K <= exp(ell)K0`, `K0V <= kV`, and `RV <= rV` bounds.

## Use the Exact Reduction

Define

```text
Z_G(s) = integral_G w_s,
Z_B(s) = integral_B w_s,
B_V(s) = integral_B w_s(f) V(tau(s,f)).
```

The weighted bad row is exactly

```text
q_V(s) = B_V(s)/((Z_G(s)+Z_B(s))V(s)).
```

Do not replace the full normalizer by an unproved constant. A sufficient free-energy route is

```text
Z_G(s) >= exp(-ell) Z_G_ref(s),
B_V(s) <= exp(-F) Z_G_ref(s)V(s),
```

which gives `q_V(s) <= exp(ell-F)`. For target `q`, require strictly `F > ell-log(q)`.
