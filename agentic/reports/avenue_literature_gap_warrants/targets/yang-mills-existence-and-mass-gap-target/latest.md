## Route A finite Wilson/OS endpoint-source table

- Avenue: `ym.routea.endpoint_source_table`
- Decision: `artifact_sprint_only`
- Allowed next modes: `artifact_sprint, falsifier_sprint, proof_delivery`
- Relevance score: `4`
- Field-impact score: `4`
- Unification score: `4`
- Route-decision score: `5`

Smallest honest claim: A finite reflected-Wilson Schur-Feshbach endpoint theorem or worked H,K collar table can isolate a same-theory OS-positivity obstruction before any continuum mass-gap claim is made.

Gap statement: The actionable gap is not generic reflection positivity. It is the missing finite endpoint-source table that would tell whether the current Wilson collar obstruction is a real same-theory artifact or only a symbolic Schur-Feshbach skeleton.

Why not already known: The cited OS and lattice literature provides reconstruction criteria and regulated gauge carriers, but the local report has not yet exhibited the explicit reflected-Wilson H,K endpoint data, sign budget, and same-theory transfer boundary needed for this route.

Closest known results:

- `ref.os-1973-1975` K. Osterwalder and R. Schrader (1973), Axioms for Euclidean Green's Functions I-II: The theorem gives a reconstruction criterion; Yang-Mills still needs Schwinger functions satisfying the hypotheses in the continuum limit.
- `ref.wilson-1974` K. G. Wilson (1974), Confinement of Quarks: Strong-coupling lattice confinement is not the same as a continuum four-dimensional Clay-problem proof.
- `ref.jaffe-witten-clay` A. Jaffe and E. Witten (2000), Quantum Yang-Mills Theory: It defines the target and admissible rigor standard; it is not a solution.
- `ref.douglas-2004` M. R. Douglas (2004), Report on the Status of the Yang-Mills Millennium Prize Problem: It is a status report, not a proof or definitive taxonomy of all later approaches.

Disqualifiers:

- `prior_art_absorbs_artifact` -> `do_not_invest_prior_art_collision`: A cited constructive-QFT or lattice gauge source already contains the same finite endpoint table and obstruction.
- `proxy_observable` -> `quarantine_low_relevance`: The calculation uses a toy matrix, non-gauge-invariant object, or symbolic collar with no Wilson carrier.
- `artifact_debt` -> `artifact_sprint_only`: The next cycle repeats the route narrative without the endpoint table, theorem, or sign/falsifier artifact.

## Representational dynamics frontier for Yang-Mills

- Avenue: `ym.representational_dynamics`
- Decision: `reframe_gap_before_investment`

- Relevance score: `4`
- Field-impact score: `5`
- Unification score: `5`
- Route-decision score: `3`

Smallest honest claim: A representational-dynamics route is worth broad investment only if it identifies a new efficient representation that preserves gauge-invariant observables, same-theory limits, OS positivity, and nontriviality witnesses better than the current Wilson/RG language.

Gap statement: The literature-aware gap is an efficient representation that simultaneously preserves gauge covariance, reflection positivity, continuum limiting data, and a nontrivial spectral carrier. The current workflow names this ambition but has not yet isolated the representation that makes the coupled requirements easier.

Why not already known: Constructive and RG sources frame lattice-to-continuum control, while the Clay target demands same-theory mass gap. The local imported overlay is a research program, not a demonstrated representation theorem that closes those coupled gates.

Closest known results:

- `ref.balaban-1985` T. Balaban (1985), Ultraviolet Stability of Three-Dimensional Lattice Pure Gauge Field Theories: Ultraviolet stability and related RG control still need same-theory continuum construction, nontriviality, and spectral-gap witnesses.
- `ref.jaffe-witten-clay` A. Jaffe and E. Witten (2000), Quantum Yang-Mills Theory: It defines the target and admissible rigor standard; it is not a solution.
- `ref.douglas-2004` M. R. Douglas (2004), Report on the Status of the Yang-Mills Millennium Prize Problem: It is a status report, not a proof or definitive taxonomy of all later approaches.

Disqualifiers:

- `programmatic_renaming` -> `reframe_gap_before_investment`: The route restates RG, OS, or Wilson requirements without a new representation object or theorem.
- `target_commitment_loss` -> `quarantine_low_relevance`: The representation loses gauge covariance, reflection positivity, nontriviality, or same-theory continuum data.
- `prior_art_absorbs_claim` -> `do_not_invest_prior_art_collision`: A cited constructive/RG theorem already supplies the same representation theorem under the same assumptions.
