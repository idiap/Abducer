# Public reasoning-trace PDF examples

An inference is auditable when the PDF lets a reader follow this chain by stable IDs:

`typed premises -> declared operator -> bounded warrant -> conclusion -> evidence or obligation -> scope and authority -> disposition -> handoff`

For a refuted contribution candidate, the conclusion remains a demotion, the evidence relation remains `refutes`, the novelty authority boundary is visible, and the unresolved prior-art obligation remains open. The renderer must not paraphrase the event into an accepted novelty claim.

For theorem delivery, the exact statement and scope precede proof obligations. A proof gap, countermodel, or failed checker is printed beside successful deductions; control completion cannot become proof acceptance.

For deep review, independent reviews and minority findings remain distinct before meta-synthesis. The report displays advisory and repair authority and explicitly excludes proof or release authority.

For Route-A, the report exposes all declared roles, specialist fan-out and join, CAS audit, same-theory checks, endpoint and margin findings, integration, and the common validation tail. Finite checks remain finite unless a separately evidenced and authorized scope transition exists.

These examples describe projection behavior, not new conclusions. The complete values and relations always come from the bound monitor packet.
