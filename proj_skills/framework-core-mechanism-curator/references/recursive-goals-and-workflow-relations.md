# Recursive goals and relations among workflows

This shared manual authoring contract applies when a paper must explain recursive goals, stopping, or how a workflow family fits together. Curators use it to freeze evidence and explanatory records; writers and reviewers use the same records. It extends conceptual-first exposition with the management of unresolved questions. It adds no registered schema, executor, fixed-runner stage, or claim that the framework implements the editorial model below.

## Evidence before an integrated model

Freeze the selected native workflow packet and relevant procedural sources with paths, spans, hashes, and source profiles. Separate native declaration, procedural prescription, editorial synthesis, constructed example, and observed execution on every proposed relationship. A compatible output/input type is a possible connection, not a declared invocation or demonstrated runtime path. Do not infer universal ordering or successful termination from graph connectivity.

Relevant source prescriptions include [contribution-oriented subgoal management](../../../skills/orchestration/contribution-oriented-subgoal-manager/SKILL.md), [completed-subgoal enablement](../../../skills/evaluation/completed-subgoal-enablement-auditor/SKILL.md), and [enablement packaging](../../../skills/reporting/subgoal-enablement-handoff-packager/SKILL.md). Resolve these through frozen evidence before attributing behavior; reading their prescriptions does not invoke their programs. Keep their contribution-maturity policy distinct from the plural theory-first inquiry in [conceptual frontier deepening](../../../skills/orchestration/conceptual-frontier-deepening/SKILL.md) and [reflective conceptual discovery](../../../skills/orchestration/reflective-conceptual-discovery/SKILL.md). In particular, a contribution score is not a universal selector of conceptual truth, and a branch's smallest publishable claim is not completion of the root scientific problem.

## Explain the recursive object

Start from why a conceptual tension creates an unresolved question. Distinguish a goal (the question or acceptance obligation) from an attempt (one bounded method for addressing it) and a workflow (a reusable procedure selected for an attempt). Several workflows may address the same goal; one workflow may be reused at several depths. A failed attempt can leave the goal open. A useful negative result can satisfy a diagnostic goal while refuting an attempted positive route.

Use a source-bound or explicitly editorial goal record containing: stable identity and revision; target, carrier, assumptions and regime; parent links and the precise contribution expected; acceptance evidence; dependency formula; candidate attempts; current evidence/unknowns; stopping and reopening conditions. Separate scheduling state, achievement status, and enablement for each parent. A shared child may help several parents differently; do not force shared obligations into a strict tree or credit them repeatedly as independent evidence.

Explain conjunctive (AND) required obligations and disjunctive (OR) alternative routes, including mixed dependency formulas. AND requires every relevant obligation plus justified composition; OR accepts an adequately supported route only if its contract is sufficient for the parent. A list of children is not itself a logical decomposition. Treat AND/OR as editorial semantics unless the frozen source declares them. Keep dependency edges distinct from revisitation edges, and prevent circular evidence from serving as its own justification.

On child return, check the exact target/version, premises, scope, evidence and residual obligations; show the map from its result to a named parent requirement. Update the parent only to the extent this return enables it. Re-evaluate the parent's own acceptance criterion. Neither child termination nor a complete child list automatically closes the parent. Useful local results, route rejection, partial enablement and parent completion require different explanations.

When assumptions, target identity or evidence change, preserve historical attempts and create a revised goal/assessment as appropriate. Identify conclusions that actually depend on changed premises and recheck or reopen their consumers transitively; retain independent results. A stale result is not thereby false. Feedback revises the problem or the method; it does not silently rewrite an earlier success into a new claim.

## Stopping and continued inquiry

Teach operational stopping separately from epistemic closure. For each live attempt explain what would justify continuing, adapting, backtracking, handing off, rejecting a route, suspending, or closing the stated goal. A stopping table must identify the triggering evidence, what stops, what remains open, and what can resume or reopen it.

- Success means the stated acceptance obligation has current adequate evidence, at its declared scope. Recheck parent enablement separately.
- A counterexample or failed transfer stops only the affected claim or route unless its scope rules out every admissible alternative. Renewed resources may resume unresolved work; they cannot rehabilitate a refuted claim under unchanged assumptions. Reopening that scientific claim requires a revised claim/context or evidence that defeats the purported falsifier, with the change stated explicitly.
- A missing indispensable input suspends or blocks the dependent attempt; record the needed input and the resumption condition.
- A fixed time, resource, depth, or attempt limit ends or suspends that allocation with an unresolved result. Budgets are control policy, never evidence that a claim is true or false. Do not promise mathematical termination of open inquiry from finite local allocations.
- Repetition without a new distinction, repaired premise, discriminating test or useful consequence prompts a documented adaptation, backtrack or no-progress stop. Preserve negative knowledge and remaining alternatives. Hard proof debt alone does not establish futility.
- A bounded useful result can end broad exploration and hand off to validation or packaging while the larger question stays open. Conceptual progress may add informative obligations; fewer open goals is not a universal progress measure.

Preserve source-specific thresholds. For example, the contribution-oriented manager's stop policy requires at least two explicit adaptation/backtracking attempts before its particular no-contribution stop; this is not a universal recursion depth or mandate to spend budget on refuted work. State operational limits and scientific adequacy criteria independently. Do not import a contribution maturity gate into another profile without an explicit editorial qualification.

## Relate the complete workflow family

Freeze the full requested workflow ID set, not a few representative names. In the current paper the historical snapshot contains 19 workflows and 65 occurrence bindings; reopen scope from sources on regeneration. Give every workflow a disposition and an exact place in a coverage table with: characteristic question, consumed/returned scientific content, related workflow IDs, relation meaning, provenance posture, and applicability/unknowns. An isolated or unresolved relationship is an honest row, not permission to invent an edge or omit that workflow.

Separate levels in prose and figures: inquiry-level orchestration, reasoning/support workflows, and development or publication controls when these are in scope. Explain composition through actual carried content and retained context. Distinguish containment, explicit invocation, artifact handoff, conceptual support, validation, alternative route, and revisitation. A workflow may occupy multiple conceptual roles; do not force all workflows into one pipeline, partition, inference-family hierarchy, or acyclic graph.

Provide a readable overview plus a complete keyed table or detail view. For structured-description requests, use separate Purpose, Input, Output and Links fields: short purpose sentence, named consumed/returned content, then relation targets and meanings. Keep epistemic status with the result and continuation with the relation. Apply the [shared presentation guidance](../../framework-paper-workflow-appendix-writer/references/step-explanation-contract.md) to goal records and steps without dropping acceptance, stopping or parent-enablement distinctions. Legends and captions distinguish source-declared links from editorial proposed handoffs and distinguish dependency from feedback. Unknown native order remains unknown. Grouping is an aid to explanation, not evidence of subtype, execution, equivalence, or successful composition. Preserve all existing occurrence-level explanations under the independent step contract.

## Running example and review

Extend the conceptual running case across a parent goal, at least one nested obligation, a meaningful alternative route, a child return and parent re-evaluation. Include a failed route or missing input, a resource/no-progress stop that leaves the scientific question unresolved, and a changed assumption that reopens affected work. Reuse actual scientific content rather than substituting task IDs and status transitions. Clearly label constructed policy and hypothetical scheduling; do not fabricate observed runs.

Review by tracing the case and map: can a reader tell why each child exists, what success establishes, which result enables which parent, what stops and why, what can restart, and how every scoped workflow participates or remains unconnected? Challenge completed-child/open-parent, exhausted-budget/open-question, stale-premise/shared-child, OR-route failure with a surviving rival, and conceptual progress with newly created obligations. Check logical sufficiency and source attribution manually, separately from counts, hashes and compilation. Record content-specific findings and repairs. Keep the conceptual argument prominent, with formal notation and the full workflow details as support; rebuild and inspect the actual requested paper before delivery.
