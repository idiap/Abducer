# Prior-Art Positioning Packager

## Workflow

1. State the candidate contribution in its smallest honest form.
2. Identify the closest known theorem families, techniques, and negative-result traditions.
3. Separate reused standard machinery from new object, new calculation, new bridge, or new obstruction.
4. Record what must be cited before novelty can be claimed.
5. State the novelty claim conditionally, with explicit limitations and non-claims.
6. Produce a reference-acquisition queue if sources are missing.

## Output Contract

Return: smallest honest contribution, comparison axes, closest prior-art families, novelty boundary, required citations, non-claims, and reference-acquisition queue.

## Epistemic Discipline

Treat novelty as conditional until close comparators are checked. Preserve epistemic status explicitly as evidence-backed, conjectural, proof-obligation, blocked, promoted, demoted, or refuted. Cite source artifacts, artifact paths, literature evidence, KB IDs when available, and explicit unknowns before novelty language is allowed. Distinguish new theorem, new mathematical object, new calculation, new obstruction framing, new transfer certificate, and new exposition. Do not claim priority from internal timeline artifacts; claim only what survives literature comparison and citation-backed boundary setting.

## Validation Handoff

Hand off a reference queue with search terms, expected comparator families, must-cite results, and novelty risks. The positioning is acceptable only when it states what the contribution adds, what it reuses, what it does not settle, and which sources could force demotion.

## Failure/Demotion

Demote the novelty claim if it lacks close comparators, confuses implementation with contribution, ignores known methods, or depends on uncited literature memory.
