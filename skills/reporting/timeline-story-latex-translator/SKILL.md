# Timeline Story LaTeX Translator

## Workflow

1. Identify the story arc: initial route, failure pressure, restrategy, current object, and decisive next test.
2. Translate the arc into physics/mathematical prose, not workflow administration.
3. Explain why abandoned or narrowed strategies did not pay off and what they taught.
4. Preserve the current strategy as a claim-bounded narrative, with explicit stop/pivot criteria.
5. End with the smallest current paper contribution and what remains blocked.

## Epistemic Discipline

State epistemic status for each story claim: evidence-backed route learning, conjectural mechanism, proof-obligation, validation-needed, blocked, promoted, demoted, or refuted. Cite source artifacts, cycle IDs, report paths, and explicit unknowns.

Do not narrate the agent workflow as if it were scientific evidence. The story is evidence only when it identifies a mathematical object, a failed assumption, a validated reduction, or a decisive falsifier.

## Output Contract

Return: LaTeX narrative section, route chronology compressed into conceptual beats, strategy-failure learning, current mathematical object, decisive next test, non-claims, source citations, epistemic status, and validation handoff.

## Failure/Demotion

Demote the section if it is bureaucratic, generic, cycle-by-cycle without conceptual compression, missing why-strategies-failed explanations, or stronger than the evidence.

## Validation Handoff

The LaTeX must compile and read as an expert scientific narrative. It must include the current object, why earlier strategies narrowed, what would count as progress, and why the paper claim remains bounded.
