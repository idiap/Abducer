# Prove Continuum Coherence

## Target

Produce continuum gauge-invariant Schwinger functions or an equivalent Euclidean theory satisfying the axioms needed for reconstruction, with a demonstrated identity as pure Yang--Mills and with regulator-independent physical normalization.

## Workflow

1. Choose a renormalized, state-separating observable algebra and test-function topology.
2. Prove uniform moment or distributional bounds and tightness for regulated correlation functionals.
3. Establish convergence, first for a core and then for the full declared observable class.
4. Prove Euclidean covariance, symmetry, locality, reflection positivity, regularity, and clustering or uniqueness at the required strength.
5. Prove nontriviality and identify the renormalized coupling or other physical scale.
6. Justify every interchange among cutoff removal, volume limit, large separation, RG depth, and observable approximation.
7. Pass the uniform rigidity estimate through the limit using a topology strong enough for positive quadratic forms.
8. Compare at least two admissible regulator families or prove a universality theorem in a precisely stated class.

## Theory-Identity Audit

Do not identify a subsequential limit as Yang--Mills from symmetry alone. Record the action or Schwinger--Dyson identity available, renormalization conditions, gauge-invariant local structure, non-Gaussianity, and universality evidence or theorem.

## Failure Modes

- Only subsequential convergence with no uniqueness or universality.
- Reflection positivity lost in the regulator or limit.
- Pointwise observable convergence too weak to pass spectral estimates.
- Limits taken in a favorable but unjustified order.
- Trivial, Gaussian, or topological limits mislabeled as four-dimensional Yang--Mills.

## Output Contract

Return the limiting objects, topology, OS-axiom ledger, theory-identity certificate, limit-order table, universality status, and inherited rigidity theorem. Mark continuum existence or regulator independence conditional wherever a required compactness, uniqueness, or comparison result is missing.
