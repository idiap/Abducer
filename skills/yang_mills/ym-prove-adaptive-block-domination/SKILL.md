## Theorem Target

For a gauge-invariant Boolean bad-block field exposed in a declared order, prove for every finite volume, site, and positive-probability history

```text
P(next block is bad | exposed history) <= q,
```

with `q` below the rooted connected-set threshold. Then apply shared-uniform sequential coupling to dominate the field by independent Bernoulli(`q`).

## Mathematical Invariants

The mathematical objects are a finite block graph `G_Lambda`, an exposure filtration `(F_i)`, bad indicators `(X_i)`, a retained boundary state, the nonlinear Yang--Mills conditional measure, and constants `q`, `alpha`, and `Delta`. Preserve these invariants throughout the argument:

- `P(X_i=1 | F_{i-1}) <= q` holds almost surely for every exposure index and every history in the support, with one `q` uniform in finite volume, admissible boundary law, and declared cutoff range.
- Conditioning is only on positive-mass histories generated by the declared filtration. The proof may not silently strengthen or weaken the sigma-algebra when passing between local charts, retained states, and microscopic links.
- Gauge covariance, locality, pure-gauge content, center/topology labels, and the declared observable algebra survive the good/charged split and every comparison map.
- Normalized good-channel bounds include chart mass, overlap multiplicity, Haar/Jacobian factors, heat-kernel prefactors, and action-remainder oscillation; pointwise energy control is not a normalized probability estimate.
- Sequential coupling preserves the coordinatewise order `X_i <= Y_i` for independent `Y_i ~ Bernoulli(q)`. Consequently every fixed connected set `C` satisfies `P(X_i=1 for i in C) <= q^|C|`.
- The convergence margin is strict: `exp(alpha) Delta^2 q < 1`. With the declared connected-animal counting convention, the reported rooted budget is `exp(alpha)q/(1-exp(alpha)Delta^2q)`.
