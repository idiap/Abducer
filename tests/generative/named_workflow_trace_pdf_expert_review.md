# Named-workflow trace PDF expert review

Review each of the six named workflows and Route-A against the generated PDF, not only its packet or TeX. A reviewer records a blocking finding when an answer is absent, ambiguous, clipped, or recoverable only from another format.

For every report:

- What changed, why, and from which typed premises and evidence?
- Which operator and public warrant connect the premises to the conclusion?
- Under which scope, regime, uncertainty, and authority does the conclusion hold?
- What was refuted, demoted, quarantined, skipped, or left as an obligation?
- Are integrity, epistemic status, and workflow disposition visibly independent?
- Where did the result go next, and what may the recipient legitimately infer?
- Can each answer be followed by stable semantic IDs into the audit appendices?

|---|---|---|
| Evidence-to-proof onramp | Can provenance, normalization, bias, grounding, and regime be reconstructed? | Bias/falsifier path and routing boundary |
| Conceptual deepening | Is frozen context separated from candidate generation and blind adjudication? | Rejected alternative and identity-blind decision |
| Theorem delivery | Are the exact statement, obligations, deductions, gaps, and countermodels distinguishable? | Proof gap or countermodel; no control-to-proof promotion |
| Contribution cultivation | Are novelty, alignment, value, and enablement independently justified? | Demotion/prior-art obligation and authority limit |
| Manuscript elaboration | Can finite, asymptotic, and continuum claims be told apart? | No unsupported scope promotion |
