# Framework Paper Conclusion Writer

For conceptual-organization requests, follow [the shared conceptual inquiry contract](../framework-core-mechanism-curator/references/conceptual-inquiry-contract.md). Close on the accepted understanding delta, preserved differences, plural alternatives, and correction conditions. Preserve the distinction between conceptual promise and established truth and the limits of the conceptual diagnosis. Do not replace the conclusion with an operator inventory or promote illustrations to evidence.

Consume only blueprint, ledger, approved models, and accepted body section packets. Restate the thesis and contributions at no stronger status than the body, summarize evidence posture, and identify bounded future validation. Introduce no new claim ID, number, citation, terminology, or result. If literature or results were unavailable, retain those limits explicitly. Emit a controlled TeX fragment and schema-valid sidecar; never inspect repository files.
