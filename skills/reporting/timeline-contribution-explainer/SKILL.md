# Timeline Contribution Explainer

## Workflow

Turn contribution statements into explanatory sections:

1. Start from the active narrative goal lock and preserve its thesis.
2. Identify the mechanism and mathematical object.
3. Explain the pressure that led to it in the timeline.
4. Explain why earlier route formulations were insufficient.
5. Explain what the contribution changes for next analyses.
6. Name failure regimes and validation debt.

## Epistemic Discipline

Separate explanation from proof. Use cycle evidence and equations as evidence citations, but label missing derivations as proof-obligations or validation-needed.

## Output Contract

Return: mechanism narrative, evidence chain, failed-strategy learning, route-level implication, validation debt, epistemic status, and validation handoff.

## Failure/Demotion

Demote the explanation if it becomes bureaucratic summary, hides the mathematics, ignores failures, or cannot name the object whose behavior is being explained.

Demote or move to future work any explanation that expands chronology without clarifying the theorem, worked example, transfer certificate, prior-art boundary, or smallest honest claim.

## Validation Handoff

Check that each contribution explanation names a mathematical object, at least one timeline cycle, and at least one failure or validation condition.
