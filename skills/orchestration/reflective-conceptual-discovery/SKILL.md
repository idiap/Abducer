# Reflective Conceptual Discovery

## Role

Make reflective creativity the primary discovery process. Generate and transform
conceptual systems, not merely isolated hypotheses, and converge on a research
programme that changes how the target problem can be understood.

Treat truth as the regulative destination and the constraint on established
claims. Do not use immediate provability or experimental convenience as the
early objective function: a fertile concept may need articulation and recursive
reflection before it yields a decisive test.

Read [references/theory-program-contract.md](references/theory-program-contract.md)
before producing a durable discovery packet.

For a major cycle, resolve and assimilate a target-scoped literature snapshot
before active portfolio selection. A bounded intuition-first pass may withhold
the detailed literature map to reduce anchoring, but every surviving candidate
must be rebased before investment, synthesis, proof routing, or novelty claims.
Seal source/version/correction and mapper/warrant policy before mapping. After
assimilation, seal a separate realization; never relabel a post-assimilation
certificate as authority over work that already occurred.

## Discovery Priorities

Apply these priorities in order:

1. essential relevance to the real target or phenomenon;
2. contact with the root conceptual tension;
3. explanatory and mechanistic understanding;
4. unification of previously separate cases, scales, routes, or constraints;
5. conceptual and philosophical depth;
6. generativity and capacity to reorganize future reasoning;
7. internal coherence and truth-aptness;
8. formal, proof, or evidential support when the theory is mature enough.

Use a relevance floor and a Pareto portfolio. Do not collapse these virtues into
one weighted score, and retain a serious challenger when candidates make
different conceptual trade-offs.

## Composition Policy

Referenced leaf skills are selective specialist routes, not a checklist. Invoke
one when its prerequisites are available and its output can materially change
the live conceptual state; otherwise record `not_applied` with a short reason.
Do not fabricate a specialist receipt or block a generative cycle merely because
an artifact-bound specialist is unavailable. The synthesizer is required only
for major multi-system reflection; a grounded `not_conceptual` diagnosis exits
before that stage.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce creativity to novelty, renamed bridges, more artifacts, or
  random variation.
- Do not assume every hard problem needs a new ontology. If the incumbent
  concepts are adequate and the bottleneck is a calculation, formalization,
  proof step, missing source, or measurement, route there explicitly instead of
  manufacturing a conceptual crisis.
- Do not reduce philosophy to mood, historical ornament, or abstract prose
  without changed primitives, distinctions, relations, mechanisms, or questions.
- Do not require an experiment for a valid conceptual advance.
- Do not let ease of measurement select the ontology or distort the target.
- Do not unify cases by deleting their decisive differences.
- Do not let a speculative theory become self-sealing; record correction
  conditions and a path toward formal or evidential constraint.
- Do not equate theoretical preference with established truth.
- Do not reduce a plural nondominated preference to the first system in a list.
- Do not let an internal or correlated judge certify philosophical depth,
  understanding, creativity, or physics value; keep those gates open until an
  authority-distinct evaluation exists.

## Mathematical Invariants

Preserve target identity, source and target object distinctions, quantifier
order, protected successes of the incumbent theory, nontriviality, known
boundary cases, physical or mathematical regime, and explicit non-claims across
conceptual transformations. Record every deliberately changed or sacrificed
invariant and why that loss might be acceptable.

## Workflow

### 1. Write the discovery charter

State the essential question, desired form of understanding, target
commitments, relevance horizon, incumbent framework, unresolved opacity, and
non-claim boundary. Distinguish a conceptual bottleneck from a merely unfinished
calculation or local proof obligation.

If the diagnosis is `not_conceptual`, return the grounded diagnosis and the
appropriate derivation, proof, reading, or empirical handoff. Do not run the
remaining creativity workflow merely to satisfy its shape.

Use `main-problem-alignment-auditor`, `physical-relevance-impact-auditor`, or
`literature-gap-relevance-auditor` when the target's relevance is unclear. Use
them to preserve the horizon, not to prune every immature idea before it can be
articulated.

### 2. Reconstruct the conceptual situation

Identify:

- current primitives, identities, distinctions, relations, operations, and
  explanatory ideals;
- inherited ontology and representation choices;
- protected successes and cases the theory already explains;
- hidden assumptions, absences, and structures the present language cannot
  express;
- the conceptual genealogy that produced the current framing.

Invoke `meta`, `kb-epistemic-hardness`, `mathematical-concept-gap-revisitor`,
`proof-depth-pressure-formalizer`, `abducer-conceptual-pressure-formalizer`, and
`abducer-failure-signal-cartographer` selectively.

### 3. Refresh and assimilate the literature frontier

Resolve the newest validated search snapshot whose target, scope, completion
time, and content digest match the active theory state. Invoke
`frontier-literature-assimilator` to compute what changed since the prior
snapshot and map each decision-relevant source into concepts, mechanisms,
barriers, assumptions, genealogy, hypotheses, and next actions.

Do not accept a global `latest` alias, a generated-at timestamp, or a source
list as evidence that a current search occurred. If no admissible current
snapshot exists, request a target-scoped refresh through the existing
literature-curation and prior-art skills. A bounded speculative cycle may run
as `literature_blind_incubation`, but it cannot claim novelty, win substantial
follow-on resources, or enter provisional synthesis until rebased.

The literature result must cause one of `spawn`, `merge`, `split`, `reframe`,
`demote`, `retire`, `reprioritize`, `new_question`, or a source-grounded
`justified_no_change`. Preserve conceptual departures from consensus when they
remain coherent and relevant; literature informs the theory space rather than
defining its limits.

Use two causally ordered authority objects. Seal a pre-map policy immediately
after acquisition and make mapping, warrant validation, and assimilation
consume it. Seal a post-assimilation realization over the exact outputs, then
make hypothesis generation, reflection, adjudication, scheduling, commitment,
and acceptance consume both. Reject late, stale, substituted, or negative-only
authority even when every digest is recomputed.

### 4. Expand the theory space

Preserve the incumbent and generate materially different conceptual systems.
For a major discovery cycle, seek at least three different transformation
families when the source material supports them; otherwise state why diversity
is constrained.

Reuse:

- `abducer-mediation-candidate-generator` for controlled transformations;
- `kb-gap-abstraction-conjecturer` and
  `efficient-representation-requirements-elicitor` for missing abstractions;
- `proof-bridge-object-forger` for new carriers and interfaces;
- `proof-creative-variant-prober` for counterfactual variations;
- `kb-abduce` and `kb-induce` for missing mechanisms and recurring structure;
- `visual-theorem-conceptualizer` for property-preserving reframing when a
  mechanism remains opaque.

Vary the conceptual architecture, not just parameter values. Consider concept
splitting, mediation, completion, new carriers, relation- or process-first
reconstruction, level changes, altered equivalence policies, inversion,
regime stratification, abstraction, and cross-system synthesis.

When a higher-level structural language may reveal a missing universal
property, compositional obstruction, equivalence policy, coherence burden, or
transport law, invoke `categorical-concept-forger` and/or
`hott-equivalence-coherence-forger`. Require an executable formal-lens receipt
and route the output back through ordinary conceptual comparison. Treat finite
categories, types, paths, equivalences, and coherences as creativity and
calibration languages. Always return the exact physical carrier, quantifiers,
regime, correction domain, falsifier, overhead, and failure witness; never infer
continuum or physical equivalence from a finite formal analogy.

For a major or stalled cycle, invoke `high-risk-hypothesis-forger`. Generate an
aggressive intuition-led portfolio across genuinely different conceptual
corners, including counter-consensus and negative theories, while preserving a
control candidate. Judge probability of truth, value if true, learning value
if false, conceptual worth, and maturation cost separately. Protect at least
two relevant nondominated wildcards when available.

Allow the forger's first pass to operate on the charter and root tensions
without detailed source mappings. Then require its second-pass literature
rebase against the frozen snapshot before selecting live theory branches.

### 5. Mature independent rivals in parallel when useful

When selected hypotheses have distinct primitives, mechanisms, or maturation
questions, invoke `parallel-hypothesis-portfolio`. Freeze the target, theory,
and literature digests; fan out branch-local workers without early cross-talk;
validate each result independently; preserve valid siblings on partial
failure; and send one conflict-preserving merge proposal to the single-writer
state reducer.

Each blind worker should articulate its own conceptual system and run a
branch-local premortem or opposed reflection without seeing sibling conclusions.
Stage 6 then performs cross-portfolio philosophical reflection and synthesis;
parallelism must not displace the reflective core.

Parallelism is optional and must follow the dependency DAG. If a newer search
snapshot appears during a wave, finish against the frozen basis, mark survivors
`literature_superseded`, and rebase them before cross-pollination, synthesis,
promotion, or further budget.

### 6. Run recursive philosophical and mathematical reflection

Invoke `reflective-theory-synthesizer` over the incumbent and rivals. Use at
least three applicable lenses, including ontological and dialectical reflection,
and require every round to cause a mutation, synthesis, demotion, or explicit
`no_change` finding.

Steelman the strongest opposition to each candidate. Ask which tensions are
resolved, preserved, displaced, or made newly intelligible. Run two to four
reflection rounds while they produce material conceptual change. After two
empty rounds, declare a plateau and reframe rather than generating paraphrases.

After parent reflection, invoke the blind conceptual preference adjudicator on
the exact ordered systems and rounds. If synthesis creates or materially changes
a candidate, adjudicate the complete resulting set again and bind that final
adjudication to the complete parent evidence, not only its digest. A committer
may replay this evidence but may not mint an unjudged integrated theory.

### 7. Articulate rival theories

For each live system state:

- primitives and ontological commitments;
- identities, differences, relations, operations, and dynamics;
- mechanism or explanatory principle;
- phenomena, theorem pressures, or route families explained;
- unification map and differences deliberately preserved;
- what becomes expressible that was previously invisible;
- implications, new questions, and formalization seeds;
- residual contradictions, limitations, and correction conditions.

A theory can remain `incubating` while these are incomplete. Do not send a seed
straight to proof or experiment merely to make it look operational.

### 8. Compare and converge reflectively

Use `abstraction-fit-gap-auditor`, `kb-coherence-evaluator`,
`bridge-object-equivalence-checker`,
`proof-conceptual-quality-heuristic-rater`, `contribution-value-assessor`, and
the relevant concept-logician, discovery-scientist, Fields-Medalist,
epistemologist, or radical-designer reviews.

Compare candidates on relevance, root-tension contact, understanding gain,
explanatory necessity, unification, preservation of difference, conceptual
depth, compression without erasure, generativity, coherence, and truth-aptness.
Record trade-offs rather than forcing a scalar winner.

Use sealed blind adjudication evidence as the sole preference and investment
authority. Preserve all members of `retain_plural_nondominated` equally. Derive
a singular convenience only for `prefer_unique`. For `revise_all`, retain the
complete nondominated set as diagnostic material, commission no support or
proof work, and emit revision, differentiation, reflection, or incubation
actions only. With no adjudication, retain diagnostic candidates without
preference authority.

Select a provisional synthesis only when it has a stable conceptual core,
reorganizes more than one target pressure, yields a precise before/after change
in understanding, generates consequential new questions or obligations, and
survives serious opposed perspectives. Retain at least one challenger or state
why none remains viable.

### 9. Build the theoretical research programmes

Build one component programme for every preferred system, preserving exact set
membership and order. Aggregate definitions, relations, mechanisms, invariants,
model classes, consequences, explanatory targets, contradictions, and proof or
derivation obligations without erasing provenance. Give a plural set parallel
programmes and support opportunities; never inherit the programme of its first
member. Give a `revise_all` set correction-indexed revision plans with
`investment_authorized=false`. Begin stricter
truth convergence here through `entailment-gap-auditor`,
`abducer-obligation-gatekeeper`, `counterexample-hunter`, and domain proof
critics, without retroactively pretending that early conceptual exploration was
already established mathematics.

### 10. Commission support probes only when theory requests them

An experiment is optional. Request a Gedanken, symbolic, proof, literature, or
simulation probe only when a mature theoretical ambiguity is named, rival
systems differ on a consequence, reflection or derivation alone cannot
currently decide it, and the result would materially revise the theory.

Prefer conceptual counterfactuals and Gedanken analysis, then exact symbolic
reasoning or finite countermodels, and only then bounded numerical simulation.
Invoke `dynamic-symbolic-simulation-experimenter` for executable support. A
simulation can constrain or calibrate a theory in its model; it cannot select an
ontology automatically or establish the target theorem.

For a controlled five-world check, seal one pre-execution commission over the
same registered premise, falsifier, spec family, frozen ancestor, generator,
schedule, and theory state. Vary only the declared adapter, model, or resource
budget. Carry the same unexecuted commission through `no_probe`; derive all
theory/proof/evidence denial flags from whole-state equality. Only the exact
registered-premise falsifier may request a later reflective review.

Return every result to reflective assimilation: ask what changed in the
conceptual system, explanation, unification map, or research question. Emit
`no_probe_needed` when further theoretical development is the right next move.

## Stage Gates

- `G0 essential_relevance`: the system addresses the real target rather than a
  tractable proxy.
- `G0L literature_rebase`: the newest admissible target-scoped search snapshot
  is frozen and assimilated, and all actively selected hypotheses have explicit
  source relations. This may remain open only during bounded intuition-first
  incubation.
- `G1 genuine_transformation`: primitives, distinctions, relations, mechanism,
  or explanatory organization changed.
- `G2 philosophical_depth`: the root aporia and the inherited frame's role in it
  are explicit. Keep this `open` under locally authored or correlated judging.
- `G3 unification`: several pressures are connected while decisive differences
  remain visible.
- `G4 understanding_gain`: a precise before/after account states what became
  intelligible. Record per-system internal structural deltas, but keep this
  `open` until authority-distinct evidence supports merit.
- `G5 reflective_resilience`: opposed perspectives caused meaningful revision
  and unresolved dissent remains visible.
- `G6 truth_apt_articulation`: the theory is non-circular, inspectable, and
  exposes consequences, correction conditions, and formal burdens.
- `G7 claim_promotion`: the relevant proof, entailment, source, or evidence gates
  license an established claim.

Use `G0`, `G0L`, and `G1` through `G6` to govern conceptual investment. Only
`G7` governs claim promotion. Experiments are not mandatory gates.

## Output Contract

Return one `reflective_conceptual_discovery_packet` using
[references/theory-program-contract.md](references/theory-program-contract.md),
including the discovery charter, conceptual genealogy, incumbent and rival
systems, frozen literature basis and assimilation delta, aggressive hypothesis
portfolio, optional parallel manifest and receipts, reflection-induced
mutations, provisional synthesis, retained challenger, unification and
understanding deltas, theory-quality Pareto comparison, theoretical programme,
truth-convergence status, optional support probe or `no_probe_needed`, complete
parent/final conceptual-adjudication evidence, ordered preferred and diagnostic
sets, one component programme and internal structural delta per retained
system, explicit investment authority, unresolved dissent, and the next
set-preserving reflective action.

## Epistemic Discipline

Use concept lifecycle states independently of truth status: `seed`,
`articulated`, `reflectively_revised`, `synthesized`,
`theoretically_preferred`, `support_ready`, `formalization_ready`, `proof_ready`,
`validated`, `refuted`, or `retired`. A concept may be deep and relevant while
remaining conjectural. Cite source artifacts, conceptual ancestors, reflection
rounds, comparison evidence, and explicit unknowns for every preferred-system
claim.

Keep conceptual promise, theoretical coherence, formal support, model support,
experimental support, and proof status separate.

## Authority Boundaries

- This skill owns the theory portfolio, reflective cycle, and provisional
  conceptual-programme recommendation.
- `frontier-literature-assimilator` owns target-scoped snapshot resolution and
  source-to-theory mappings, not theory preference or truth promotion.
- `high-risk-hypothesis-forger` owns aggressive intuition-led seed composition,
  not selection as true or established.
- `parallel-hypothesis-portfolio` owns frozen fan-out, branch validation, and a
  merge proposal; only the single-writer reducer commits shared theory state.
- Specialist creativity and review skills own local transformations and
  judgments; no single score chooses the theory.
- `reflective-theory-synthesizer` owns mutation-producing philosophical
  reflection and candidate synthesis, not preference or final promotion.
- `blind-conceptual-preference-adjudicator` owns the sealed set-valued internal
  preference relation. It does not establish independent depth, understanding,
  creativity, truth, generalization, or physics value.
- `categorical-concept-forger` and `hott-equivalence-coherence-forger` own
  bounded formal-lens transformations and exact physics handbacks, not continuum
  construction, physical equivalence, theorem status, or automatic preference.
- Experiment skills answer theory-requested support questions and cannot commit
  conceptual preference or theorem status.
- A durable state reducer records accepted state events but does not compute
  conceptual worth mechanically.

## Codex Exec Protocol

Follow `docs/reference/codex-exec-skill-protocol.md` when local artifacts can ground the
charter, genealogy, pressure, comparison, or support probe. Inspect produced
artifacts before interpretation. A generative conceptual synthesis does not
require a deterministic command; when no execution is appropriate, state that
explicitly and cite the theoretical inputs and unknowns.

## Failure/Demotion

Demote novelty theatre, philosophical vapour, unification by erasure,
measurement-driven ontology, proxy drift, self-sealing theories, premature
convergence, endless reflection without mutation, and speculative fertility
that never tightens toward formal obligations. Also demote creativity overreach
when ordinary work in the incumbent framework is sufficient. Quarantine any established claim
that lacks its `G7` authority while preserving promising theory seeds at their
honest lifecycle state.

## Validation Handoff

End with the next theory action—reflect, reframe, differentiate, synthesize,
formalize, derive, compare, read, prove, or optionally probe—and the condition
that would change the preferred theory programme.
