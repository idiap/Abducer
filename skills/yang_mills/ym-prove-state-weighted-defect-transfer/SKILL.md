```text
integral V(sigma') Q(sigma,d sigma') <= q_V V(sigma)
```

## Mathematical Invariants

The mathematical objects are a retained state space `S`, a positive sub-Markov kernel `Q`, a measurable Lyapunov weight `V:S->[1,infinity)`, a root law `mu`, and constants `q_V`, `alpha`, and `Delta`. Preserve:

- The pointwise weighted drift inequality `QV(sigma) <= q_V V(sigma)` for every state in the declared support, with one `q_V` uniform in volume, admissible boundary law, and cutoff range.
- Kernel positivity: applying `Q` preserves inequalities, so induction gives `Q^nV <= q_V^n V`. No cancellation-based estimate may be substituted for this positive-kernel implication.
- The initial moment `integral V dmu <= M` is finite with `M` uniform in the declared root states; the weight may not hide an unbounded entry cost.
- The strict cluster invariant `exp(alpha) Delta^2 q_V < 1` is checked with the same graph and connected-animal convention used in the final sum.
- Retained-state sufficiency, gauge covariance, locality, reflection compatibility, center, cut-locus/orbit, topology labels, and observable content survive the quotient, transition, and good/charged decomposition.
- Finite Perron--Frobenius optimization may choose `V` only from the declared kernel. Neither `V` nor `Q` may depend on the desired continuum or spectral-gap conclusion.
