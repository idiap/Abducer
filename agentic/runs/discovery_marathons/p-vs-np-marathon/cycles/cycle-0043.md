# Generated-OR residual cap gate for hardness magnification

## Candidate Lemma

Residual-cap gate: if B_N(s,E)<=N^b for one-sided generated-range caps of size s=N^sigma and max{rho,b+1+sigma,1+delta}<1+epsilon with E=N^delta, then C(H_N)<N^{1+epsilon-o(1)}. Thus any valid source lower bound must refute such residual caps.

## Definitions

- source object: H_N(x)=OR_{j<=M} SAT(F_{N,j}(x)), M=N^q, L=(log N)^d, with all F_{N,j} generated by a shared circuit G_N of size N^rho.
- target object: SAT on L-bit formulas against SIZE(L^c) for every fixed c; a valid H_N lower bound plus the bridge would imply SAT notin P/poly.
- controlled interface: polylog-query generated OR; SAT circuits are substituted only inside generated formulas, while source leakage is audited by one-sided residual caps.
- target-preserving condition: d>1 and q,rho<1+epsilon; G_N is shared, the SAT-query length stays polylogarithmic, and no SAT truth-table advice is introduced.
- B_N(s,E) is the least B such that every residual positive set R with |R|>E has a one-sided cap of size s hitting at least |R|/B points.

## Assumptions

- Caps are one-sided: cap(x)=1 implies H_N(x)=1 on the generated range.
- The cap circuit may use nonuniform advice about G_N, so the audit is against leakage, not only uniform algorithms.
- Equality patches for E missed positive inputs cost O(EN) gates.
- Parameters satisfy q in ((1+epsilon)/2,1+epsilon), d>1, rho<1+epsilon.

## Equations

- target_substitution: `C(H_N) \le N^{\max\{\rho,q\}+o(1)}`. Valid when: Valid when SAT_L has L^c-size nonuniform circuits for fixed c, L=(log N)^d with d>1, and G_N is shared once.
- residual_greedy_cover: `K \le \lceil B_N(s,E)(N\ln 2+1)\rceil`. Valid when: Valid when every residual positive set larger than E has a one-sided cap hitting at least a 1/B_N(s,E) fraction.
- cap_upper_bound: `C(H_N) \le N^\rho+O(K(s+L^{O(1)})+EN)`. Valid when: Applies after caps are one-sided on the generated range, share G_N, and the remaining E positive inputs are patched by equality tests.

## Attempted Derivation

- Assume the generated-OR carrier and the target bridge max{rho,q}<1+epsilon are fixed.
- Define B_N(s,E) by adversarial residual positives, not by the first cap or an average distribution.
- Apply greedy covering: residual size shrinks by a factor at most 1-1/B_N until at most E positives remain.
- OR the K caps and patch the E misses; comparing exponents gives the threshold b+1+sigma<1+epsilon and delta<epsilon.

Blocking gap: No known method proves worst-case residual cap denominator B_N(s,E)>N^{epsilon-sigma-o(1)} for semantic one-sided caps with nonuniform advice.

## Falsifiers

- Residual-cap falsifier: s=N^sigma, E=N^delta, B_N(s,E)=N^b with b+1+sigma<1+epsilon and delta<epsilon gives small circuits for H_N.
- Target-threshold falsifier: q>=1+epsilon or rho>=1+epsilon prevents SAT substitution from contradicting an N^{1+epsilon} source lower bound.
- Nonuniform-leakage falsifier: caps may be chosen after seeing G_N and the generated range; random term entropy is invalid unless it holds for adversarial residual sets.
- Naturalness probe: a dense constructive property excluding all small caps risks the natural-proofs obstruction, so the certificate must be non-natural or distribution-local.

## Learning

Cycle 43 converts the cap profile into a residual fractional covering threshold. A valid source lower bound must rule out greedy one-sided cap covers with B_N(s,E)<N^{epsilon-sigma-o(1)}; absence of a large first cap is not enough.

## Gedanken Experiment Design Seed

- Need decision: `design_now_as_falsifier_probe`
- Rationale: The route now hinges on residual cap denominators; another entropy restatement will not decide whether one-sided semantic caps greedily cover positives.
- Situation seed: High-q generated-OR carrier with L=(log N)^2, q=(1+epsilon)/2+epsilon/4, rho<1+epsilon/4, and formulas produced by a planted-or-random 3CNF generator.
- Varied variable: The residual cap exponent b where B_N(s,E)=N^b for polylog-size one-sided caps and E=N^{epsilon/2}.
- Held-fixed controls: Target bridge max{rho,q}<1+epsilon remains fixed., SAT-query length is polylogarithmic with d=2., Caps must be one-sided on the generated range., Equality patch budget is fixed at E=N^{epsilon/2}.
- Expected observation: If b<epsilon-o(1), greedy caps collapse the source lower bound; if every adversarial residual forces b>=epsilon-o(1), the source audit survives this falsifier.
- Transfer certificate needed: A theorem upgrading cap behavior to worst-case nonuniform residual sets, or an explicit cap construction giving the valid circuit upper bound.

# Barrier-Aware Lower-Bound Route: time-bounded Kolmogorov transfer gate

## Candidate Lemma

A near-enumeration time-bounded Kolmogorov lower bound for the SAT truth table transfers quantitatively to a same-slice nonuniform circuit lower bound, with threshold Theta(s log s) bits and time Theta(N s polylog s).

## Definitions

- source object: x_m=TT(SAT_m), the N=2^m bit truth table of SAT on m-bit encodings, measured by K_U^T(x_m) for a fixed universal deterministic printer U.
- target object: nonuniform Boolean circuits SIZE(m^k) deciding SAT on every m-bit encoding, with malformed formulas mapped to 0.
- controlled interface: a size-s circuit C is encoded as a counted program that enumerates all y in {0,1}^m and prints C(y) in lexicographic order.
- target-preserving condition: the decompressor time bound T(N) is at least the circuit-enumeration time O(N s polylog s) and the program length counts all circuit and advice bits.
- K_U^T(x) is the minimum length of a U-program printing exactly x within T(|x|) steps.
- The source model is exact-output time-bounded description complexity, not MCSP membership and not a randomized distinguisher.

## Assumptions

- U is fixed before the lower-bound statement; machine invariance losses are additive constants only.
- Circuit descriptions of size s have length at most A s log s under a standard gate-list encoding.
- The decompressor has no SAT oracle, no hidden advice, and no nonuniform table except the counted program bits.
- The transfer is same-slice: an m-input circuit for SAT_m must generate the length-N truth table x_m.

## Equations

- circuit_to_description_upper_bound: `SAT_m \in SIZE(s) \Rightarrow K_U^{B N s\log s}(x_m) \le A(s\log s+\log m),\quad N=2^m.`. Valid when: Valid when U is fixed, the circuit encoding is explicit, and T covers lexicographic evaluation of all N inputs.
- contrapositive_size_threshold: `K_U^{B N m^k\log m}(x_m)>A m^k\log m \Rightarrow SAT_m \notin SIZE(m^k).`. Valid when: Valid when the lower-bound statement uses the same U, the same slice length m, and the same enumeration-time budget.
- uniform_consequence_if_repeated: `[\forall k\ \exists^\infty m:\ K_U^{B_k N m^k\log m}(x_m)>A_k m^k\log m]\Rightarrow SAT\notin P/poly.`. Valid when: Valid when the high-K claim holds infinitely often for each circuit exponent and constants absorb uniform simulation overhead.

## Attempted Derivation

- Fix m, N=2^m, and x_m=TT(SAT_m).
- Assume a size-s Boolean circuit C decides SAT_m on all m-bit encodings.
- Encode m and the gate list of C using O(s log s+log m) bits.
- A universal program enumerates all y in {0,1}^m, evaluates C(y), and prints the resulting bit; total time is O(N s polylog s).
- Therefore K_U^{O(Ns polylog s)}(x_m)<=O(s log s+log m).
- Taking the contrapositive yields the same-slice circuit lower bound at threshold l=Theta(s log s).
- If the high-K statement holds i.o. for every s=m^k, then SAT has no polynomial-size nonuniform circuit family, which would be stronger than needed for P != NP.

Blocking gap: No known method proves K_U^{O(N m^k polylog m)}(TT(SAT_m))>Theta(m^k log m) for explicit SAT. If such a method supplies a poly(N)-constructive large certificate, it becomes a natural-proof object; if it relativizes fully, an oracle-collapse falsifier should apply after oracle-circuit compression is formalized.

## Falsifiers

- falsifier: If T(N)>=C N^2 polylog N, brute-force SAT enumeration gives K_U^T(TT(SAT_m))=O(log m), so the proposed high-K source claim is false.
- falsifier: For PadSAT with core length n=c log m, brute-force core enumeration runs in N m^{c+O(1)}, collapsing any target transfer for k at least c+O(1).
- falsifier: A claimed escape that proves a poly(N)-constructive large property containing SAT and excluding SIZE(m^k) is a standard natural proof, not a barrier-avoiding argument.
- falsifier: A fully relativizing high-K proof would conflict with a BGS-style oracle where the corresponding relativized SAT has small oracle circuits, once oracle queries are counted in the compression interface.
- falsifier: Hidden advice in the decompressor invalidates the source model because the program length no longer measures only the transferred circuit description.

## Learning

Cycle 43 isolates a valid quantitative bridge: K_U^{O(N m^k polylog m)}(TT(SAT_m))>Theta(m^k log m) implies SAT_m notin SIZE(m^k). The same route fails if T is too generous, since brute-force SAT printing gives O(log m) descriptions by time about N^2, and padded cores create sharper collapse thresholds. The barrier status is precise: high-K is large and useful, but not poly(N)-constructive at polynomial circuit thresholds; a constructive proof would naturalize. This is route-level progress, not evidence by itself for P != NP.

- Rationale: The bridge is quantitatively valid only in a narrow decompression-time window; padding can destroy the source lower bound without touching the formal circuit-to-compression implication.

# MCSP / Meta-Complexity Carrier Transfer

## Candidate Lemma

For a nonempty truth-table invariant used as an MCSP no-side carrier, universal soundness at threshold N^epsilon requires minimum accepted support z_m=Omega(N^epsilon/m). Thus low ANF degree, low positive one-certificate width, DNF accepting terms with few positive literals, or shallow accepting transcripts are quantitative falsifiers for the source.

## Definitions

- source object: a family A_m subseteq {0,1}^N with N=2^m, E-uniform decidability/canonicalization, and zero-fill vulnerability z_m=min{|supp(T)|: A_m(T)=1}.
- target object: unrestricted Boolean circuit lower bound Ckt(T)>N^epsilon used as an MCSP no-side row, later padded through kappa-density and distinct-image entropy D to an E lower bound.
- controlled interface: acceptance A_m(T)=1 may enter the carrier transfer only as a sound assertion that T is a hard truth table in the unrestricted nonuniform circuit model.
- target-preserving condition: the predicate is nonempty, advice-free, and universally sound for all accepted tables, or else an E-uniform selector must output a certified hard accepted table without changing kappa, D, or epsilon.
- positive one-certificate width pc^+_1(A_m) is the minimum number of coordinates forced to 1 in any genuine one-certificate for A_m.
- ANF degree deg_2(A_m) is the degree of the multilinear F_2 polynomial representing the Boolean predicate A_m over truth-table bits.

## Assumptions

- N=2^m and T is the full truth table of an m-input Boolean function.
- A_m is nonempty and every accepted T is claimed to satisfy Ckt(T)>s_m with s_m=N^epsilon.
- Circuits are unrestricted nonuniform Boolean circuits; equality to one m-bit address has size O(m).
- For the ANF clause, A_m is identified with its multilinear polynomial over F_2.

## Equations

- zero_fill_bounds: `z_m=\min_{A_m(T)=1}|\operatorname{supp}(T)|\le \min\{\deg_2(A_m),\operatorname{pc}^{+}_1(A_m)\}`. Valid when: Valid when A_m is nonempty, pc^+_1 counts positive literals in true one-certificates, and the ANF bound uses the F_2 multilinear form.
- sparse_table_upper_bound: `\operatorname{Ckt}(T_z)\le c_0 m z_m+c_0`. Valid when: Valid when T_z has support z_m and unrestricted circuits may hardwire the accepted input addresses using equality tests.
- soundness_threshold: `z_m\ge \Omega(N^\epsilon/m)=2^{\epsilon m-O(\log m)}`. Valid when: Valid when A_m is claimed to be universally sound for MCSP no-side threshold N^epsilon against unrestricted nonuniform circuits.
- syntactic_threshold_collapse: `\deg_2(A_m)\le N^\pi\ \text{or}\ \operatorname{pc}^{+}_1(A_m)\le N^\pi\quad \Rightarrow\quad \epsilon\le \pi+o(1)`. Valid when: Valid when the syntactic upper bound holds on infinitely many source lengths where the carrier asserts constant-exponent MCSP hardness.

## Attempted Derivation

- Choose an accepted table T_z of minimum support z_m for the proposed invariant A_m.
- Compute T_z by an OR of equality tests to the z_m positive addresses; this gives unrestricted circuit size O(z_m m).
- Universal MCSP no-side soundness at threshold N^epsilon therefore implies z_m=Omega(N^epsilon/m).
- If A_m has a one-certificate C with p positive literals, the zero completion of C is accepted and has support at most p, so p must satisfy the same lower bound.
- If deg_2(A_m)=d and A_m is nonzero, vanishing on all inputs of weight at most d would force every ANF coefficient of degree at most d to vanish by Mobius induction; hence some accepted input has weight at most d.
- Combining the previous two bullets gives the syntactic threshold collapse epsilon<=pi+o(1) for degree or positive-certificate exponent pi.
- Compose only after this audit with the earlier valid extraction delta<epsilon/(kappa(1+D)).

Blocking gap: The audit supplies necessary conditions, not a source theorem: we still need a bounded-D, cap-compatible, non-natural, advice-free invariant with z_m and positive one-certificate complexity at least N^epsilon/m and with actual unrestricted row hardness.

## Falsifiers

- falsifier: minimum accepted support z_m below N^epsilon/O(m), since the sparse accepted table has an O(z_m m) unrestricted circuit.
- falsifier: ANF degree N^pi with pi<epsilon on infinitely many source lengths, by the Mobius low-weight accepted input argument.
- falsifier: an accepting DNF term, decision-tree leaf, or proof transcript with subthreshold positive width.
- falsifier: a verifier whose table-level acceptance is only existential over contaminated easy rows and lacks E-uniform hard-row selection.
- falsifier: nonuniform length selection or quotient unranking that hides the hard row in advice.
- falsifier: claiming high ANF degree alone while allowing small accepted support or small positive one-certificates.

## Learning

Cycle 43 adds a necessary source parameter: zero-fill vulnerability. High ANF degree is not the real invariant; any accepted sparse table or small positive accepting certificate gives an explicit small unrestricted circuit and destroys MCSP no-side soundness. This is route-level progress and a concrete falsifier family, not evidence for P != NP itself.
