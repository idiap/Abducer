# High-Risk Hypothesis Forger

## Role

Create serious conceptual leaps, not random novelty or slightly renamed proof
routes. Optimize first for essential relevance, possible understanding gain,
unification power, and the creation of new theoretical affordances. Treat high
risk as a reason for honest incubation, not automatic rejection.

Use `agentic.discovery.aggressive_provider_search` as the discovery-grade
execution path. Read
[references/high-risk-hypothesis-contract.md](references/high-risk-hypothesis-contract.md)
for hypothesis content guidance. Read
[references/conceptual-corner-atlas.md](references/conceptual-corner-atlas.md)
only when constructing the matched structural control or the precommitted copy
corpus. Never use that atlas to seed, label, or quota the experimental provider
slots. Treat the executable module and
`agentic/spec/aggressive_provider_search_execution_v1.schema.json` as
authoritative when an older reference describes the legacy fixed-atlas study.

## Required Inputs

Require a discovery charter, root tensions, incumbent conceptual systems,
protected successes, forbidden target drift, current theory-state version, and
an exact current literature projection and bundle. Also require a
target-predeclared finite coverage problem, a commitment to the known taxonomy,
a precommitted known-template corpus, at least two provider identities and two
model identities, and replayable generation and maturation workers.

Block the discovery-grade route when the literature authority cannot be reopened
at the governed present-use time. Permit literature-blind thought only as an
explicitly non-authoritative incubation outside this execution path.

## Canonical Executable Route

Construct target atoms from the root contradictions and protected successes,
then call `build_target_predeclared_coverage_problem` before requesting any
hypothesis. Predeclare finite admissible signature cells over:

- intervention kind;
- prediction direction;
- correction kind;
- mechanism-graph topology;
- relation polarity;
- physics-handback status.

Do not derive this space from returned candidates. Do not put programme names,
corner-family labels, desired classifications, or fixed proposal templates in
provider requests.

Call `run_aggressive_provider_search` with 8–12 precommitted slots and equal
opportunity budgets. Require this role schedule before outputs exist:

- one first-slot `capable_conservative_control`;
- exactly two `protected_wildcard` slots;
- five to nine `aggressive_candidate` slots.

Declare provider and model plurality honestly. Never infer provider, model,
human, cognitive, semantic, or evaluator independence from distinct identifiers,
process isolation, blinding, or parallel execution.

Run both phases as actual bounded, killable process waves:

1. Run all author branches concurrently and preserve accepted, invalid, failed,
   and timed-out terminal receipts.
2. Freeze accepted raw inventions with the known taxonomy withheld by
   commitment.
3. Give each maturation branch only its own raw invention and the digests—not
   content—of sibling inventions.
4. Run every ready maturation branch concurrently and record
   `dependency_blocked` receipts for authors that did not survive.
5. Reap every process group; do not emulate parallelism with a sequential loop
   or ask one provider to role-play multiple branches.

Bind the exact transitive current-literature input into every author and
maturation request. Reopen projection currentness at validation time; do not
silently reuse a once-current snapshot.

## Conceptual Generation

Temporarily suspend demands for immediate proof, experiment, consensus, or local
tractability. Preserve target identity, non-circularity, protected successes,
and a concrete physics handback. Treat experiments as later support for
theory-driven discovery, never as the generator or primary selector here.

For every raw invention, state the intuition being trusted—such as symmetry,
analogy, mechanism, invariance, impossibility, emergence, compression, aesthetic
tension, or historical anomaly—without choosing from a required authored menu.
For every matured invention, make the leap intelligible through complete
semantic content: new primitives and relations, mechanism steps, target
pressure, protected success, sacrificed assumption, possible unification,
correction condition, countermodel or falsifier, and physics-facing decision
handback.

Let providers discover conceptual corners rather than self-declare families.
Require the typed evidence needed to reconstruct each family from complete
content: intervention, prediction, correction, mechanism graph, relation
polarity, falsifier binding, covered target atoms, and physics return.

Reuse `kb-abduce`, `abducer-mediation-candidate-generator`,
`kb-gap-abstraction-conjecturer`, `proof-bridge-object-forger`,
`proof-creative-variant-prober`, `gedanken-counterfactual-variant-builder`,
`review-reasoner-radical-designer`, and `reflective-theory-synthesizer`
selectively inside isolated branches. Let those skills perform local operations;
keep provider-plural portfolio composition and causal receipts in this skill.

After articulation, use `failed-hypothesis-anticipator` for a premortem that
names the earliest collapse, missing carrier, proxy shift, or hidden transfer
without rejecting a branch merely for difficulty. Route proof-ready descendants
to `kb-novel-proof-strategist`. Use `kb-hypothesis-evaluator` and
`kb-hypothesis-refutation-loop` only after stable IDs and new KB or formal
evidence exist; never let their lexical or structural scores determine immature
conceptual worth.

## Blind Reconstruction and Copy Attacks

Reconstruct semantic families only after maturation. Strip slot, provider,
genealogy, invention, and self-awarded role metadata from the complete content
presented to the assessor. Derive the family signature from bound typed evidence
and graph topology, then keep the private alias-to-invention binding separate.
Reject attempts to inject family labels; do not mistake exact content identity
for paraphrase-invariant conceptual identity.

Precommit the legacy fixed-template/structural atlas as a matched control and
copy-attack corpus only. Give the capable conservative control the same budget
and require a task-specific decision-changing physics handback. Compare it
descriptively under the same content representation. Never use the atlas as the
experimental generator, a family-coverage authority, evidence of creative
advantage, or a source of hidden provider labels.

Run exact and identity-erased content/evidence similarity attacks against the
precommitted corpus. Quarantine exact or high-similarity template copies from
coverage. Retain their receipts and assessment evidence so failure cannot be
erased by filtering.

## Coverage and Retention

Require a candidate witness with exactly six distinct eligible, non-control,
non-copy reconstructed family cells covering every predeclared target atom when
the target problem is satisfiable. Do not relax the minimum because providers
failed to produce coverage.

Authorize fewer than six only when the target-predeclared finite problem itself
is UNSAT and `solve_target_predeclared_coverage` returns a verified
inclusion-minimal unsatisfiable core plus finite countermodel. Precommit this
problem before generation. Never construct an UNSAT claim from the observed
portfolio, provider failure, or an inconvenient family count.

Retain all copy-eligible matured candidates, both protected wildcards, and the
capable control. Keep probability of being right, value if right, learning value
if wrong, maturation cost, literature relation, and formal burden separate. Do
not use a scalar, Pareto rank, confidence, lexical novelty, or truth estimate to
prune the discovery-grade execution.

## Validation and Handoff

Return one schema-valid `aggressive_provider_search_execution`. Validate it with
`validate_aggressive_provider_search_execution`, reopening literature
currentness and replaying the request, slot schedule, terminal receipts,
pre-taxonomy freeze, maturation isolation, blind family reconstruction,
copy-control result, finite SAT or minimal-UNSAT certificate, retention policy,
and causal order.

End with the retained branch aliases, two protected wildcards, capable control,
literature authority, copy quarantines, coverage witness or target countermodel,
first maturation questions, and expected conceptual payoffs. Hand only valid
descendants to reflective synthesis, proof, simulation, or theory-supporting
experiment lanes; do not claim that execution directly changed a theory
preference, proof schedule, experiment schedule, or physics decision.

Preserve null authority for novelty, creativity, merit, relevance, conceptual
depth, philosophical understanding, unification, literature benefit, physics
value, truth, and proof, as well as provider, model, human, cognitive, semantic,
and evaluator independence. Treat provider plurality, process isolation,
full-content blinding, and verified coverage as narrower procedural facts only.

## Guardrails

- Aggressive does not mean uncited, incoherent, mystical, or target-free.
- Do not require a cheap falsifier before a concept is articulate enough to
  have consequences.
- Do not call lexical novelty conceptual distance.
- Do not import a cross-field analogy without preserved and broken invariants.
- Do not let a literature collision erase a genuinely different mechanism.
- Do not promote intuition to truth status.
- Do not treat a passed process contract as creative superiority or scientific
  support.
- Do not apply a lexical or proof-hypothesis evaluator as the primary selector
  for an immature conceptual portfolio.

## Mathematical Invariants

Preserve the target statement, nontriviality, quantifier and regime structure,
protected incumbent successes, and every declared source/target distinction.
Name each assumption or invariant deliberately broken by the leap and include a
boundary case where the hypothesis becomes vacuous, circular, or merely a proxy.

## Epistemic Discipline

Record epistemic status for the intuition, literature relation, conceptual
articulation, formal burden, and truth claim separately. Cite the evidence,
source artifacts, KB IDs, and explicit unknowns that ground protected successes,
known barriers, and literature collisions; intuition provenance is not evidence.

Use lifecycle or maturation labels only after blind family reconstruction and
outside provider-authored content. Keep truth probability, conceptual worth,
literature status, proof status, and model support separate.

## Codex Exec Protocol

Follow `docs/reference/codex-exec-skill-protocol.md` when repository artifacts ground the
charter, tensions, prior failures, or literature input. Use the executable route
when callbacks and exact inputs are available. State explicitly when execution
is unavailable and do not substitute an authored static portfolio while calling
it provider search.

## Failure/Demotion

Demote proxy drift, novelty theatre, analogy without transport, self-sealing
claims, conceptual corners that collapse to the same mechanism, and high reward
asserted without an understanding or unification path. Incubate rather than
reject a relevant deep seed whose formal consequences are not yet mature. Test
at least one edge case where the proposed mechanism becomes a proxy, tautology,
or disguised incumbent theory.
