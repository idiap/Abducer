## Active Route Interpretations

### candidate.99d7c53a3321

- Route family: `Balaban RG continuum-control route`
- Verdict: substantive route, not proof
- Thesis: The route treats Balaban-style renormalisation group control as a possible constructive existence spine: scale-by-scale effective actions must remain stable while the ultraviolet cutoff is removed, producing a nontrivial continuum Yang-Mills measure or Schwinger-function family.
- Physical meaning: If successful, the physics content is not merely ultraviolet regularity; it is confinement-like suppression of long-range gauge-invariant correlations in a genuine four-dimensional interacting quantum gauge field.
- Load-bearing bridge: The decisive bridge is from RG-controlled cutoff measures to OS-positive continuum Schwinger functions, and then from those functions to a reconstructed theory with a positive excitation threshold.
- Conceptual bottleneck: Balaban estimates are strong evidence for ultraviolet control, but the Clay problem also requires nontriviality, OS/Wightman adequacy, and a positive mass gap in the same limiting theory. The route fails if those are proved in separate regulated worlds.

Mathematical objects:
- regulated lattice or continuum-cutoff Yang-Mills measures for compact simple gauge group G
- Balaban effective actions and polymer/small-field estimates across scales
- candidate Schwinger functions obtained after cutoff removal
- gauge-invariant observables whose correlations can witness nontriviality and a gap

Mechanism chain:
1. establish uniform multiscale RG bounds for effective actions
2. use those bounds to obtain tightness or convergence of regulated measures/Schwinger functions
3. prove OS positivity and Euclidean invariance survive the limiting procedure
4. separate the limiting object from a Gaussian or empty theory by an observable witness
5. derive a cutoff-stable mass-gap witness, such as exponential decay or a Hamiltonian spectral lower bound

False-positive controls:
- Do not count finite-volume, fixed-cutoff, or infrared-cutoff control as continuum existence.
- Do not count Gaussian/free limiting behavior as Yang-Mills nontriviality.
- Do not infer a mass gap from RG stability unless the gap witness survives cutoff removal.

Next lemma questions:
- Which Balaban effective-action estimates are uniform enough to define a subsequential OS-positive limit?
- What gauge-invariant observable rules out collapse to a trivial continuum measure?
- Can the same RG bounds provide exponential decay, or is a separate infrared/confinement argument needed?

Why this is not a proof:

This interpretation identifies a mathematically meaningful proof programme, but none of the listed load-bearing lemmas has been discharged inside the artifact.

### candidate.3e9a722a8615

- Route family: `Euclidean correlator-decay to spectral-gap route`
- Verdict: substantive route, not proof
- Thesis: The route interprets Euclidean exponential decay of gauge-invariant correlators as a spectral gap programme: reflection positivity should turn Euclidean time decay into a transfer-matrix or Hamiltonian lower bound after OS reconstruction.
- Physical meaning: The physics content is a rigorous version of massive behavior: gauge-invariant excitations cannot approach the vacuum with arbitrarily small energy, so long-distance correlations decay exponentially.
- Load-bearing bridge: The decisive bridge is decay-to-spectrum transfer: exponential Euclidean correlator decay must become a positive Hamiltonian gap in the reconstructed continuum theory, not merely in a lattice approximation.
- Conceptual bottleneck: Strong-coupling lattice decay is not enough. The hard step is uniformity through the continuum limit while preserving reflection positivity, nontriviality, and control over the physical observable sector.

Mathematical objects:
- reflection-positive Schwinger functions for gauge-invariant Yang-Mills observables
- transfer matrix or reconstructed Hamiltonian generated by Euclidean time translations
- vacuum sector and vacuum-orthogonal excitation subspace
- cutoff-uniform exponential decay constants for selected correlation functions

Mechanism chain:
1. identify Schwinger functions or local gauge-invariant observables carrying the decay claim
2. prove reflection positivity and construct the transfer matrix/Hilbert space
3. establish exponential Euclidean time decay with constants independent of lattice spacing and cutoffs
4. pass through OS reconstruction to Wightman fields and a positive-energy Hamiltonian
5. show the decay lower-bounds the first non-vacuum excitation and therefore proves a spectral gap Delta > 0

False-positive controls:
- Reject decay estimates whose constants vanish as the cutoff is removed.
- Reject transfer-matrix gaps that live only in a regulated lattice Hilbert space.
- Reject observables that do not constrain the physical Hamiltonian spectrum after OS reconstruction.

Next lemma questions:
- Which gauge-invariant Schwinger functions are sufficient to detect the vacuum-orthogonal spectral threshold?
- Can reflection positivity be preserved for the exact limiting objects used in the decay argument?
- What uniform decay estimate survives the lattice-continuum passage without becoming vacuous?

Why this is not a proof:

This interpretation identifies a mathematically meaningful proof programme, but none of the listed load-bearing lemmas has been discharged inside the artifact.

## Cross-Route Synthesis

- Constructive spine: The Balaban RG route supplies the strongest continuum-construction pressure, while the correlator-decay route supplies the clearest spectral-gap transfer pressure.
- Missing joint step: The central missing joint step is to make the continuum object, OS reconstruction, nontriviality witness, and positive spectral gap live in the same limiting Yang-Mills theory.

Next mathematical-physics focus:
- Treat Balaban/RG control as progress only when it outputs OS-positive continuum Schwinger functions for gauge-invariant observables.
- Treat correlator decay as progress only when the decay rate is cutoff-uniform and its spectral measure belongs to a determining observable family.
- State the bridge lemma that could let RG-controlled construction feed the Euclidean spectral-measure argument in one limiting Yang-Mills theory.
