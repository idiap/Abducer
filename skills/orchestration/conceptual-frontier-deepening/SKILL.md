# Conceptual Frontier Deepening

## Role

Deepen the conceptual frontier before proof optimization or experimental
search. Treat reflective creativity, essential relevance, unification with
preserved difference, conceptual depth, philosophical illumination, and
truth-aptness as the central research problem.

This skill coordinates existing capabilities. It does not create a second
discovery system and it does not turn local protocol completion into evidence
that an idea is creative, true, deep, physically valuable, or superior.

## Reuse graph

Reuse these skills and runtimes in this order:

1. `frontier-literature-assimilator` and the raw-capture/semantic bridge for
   the newest bounded target literature, corrections, supersessions,
   retractions, warrants, and explicit coverage limits.
2. `literature-conditioned-invention-forger` for the exact pre-taxonomy
   literature-conditioned invention boundary and semantic counterfactuals.
3. `high-risk-hypothesis-forger` plus
   `agentic.discovery.aggressive_provider_search` for actual target-specific
   parallel authors, protected wildcards, a capable conservative control,
   independent family reconstruction, and verified bounded coverage.
4. `parallel-hypothesis-portfolio` and
   `agentic.discovery.open_world_creativity` for isolated maturation,
   complete branch retention, conflict-preserving aggregation, and hard
   integrity receipts.
5. `reflective-conceptual-discovery` for ontology, dialectic, genealogy,
   modality, unification, and philosophy-of-physics transformations over the
   complete conceptual core.
6. `blind-conceptual-preference-adjudicator` plus
   `agentic.discovery.route_neutral_semantic_evaluation` for complete-content,
   route-neutral, counterbalanced, plural judgment.
7. `central-formal-conceptual-routes` when Category theory or HoTT can expose
   a mediator, equivalence, transport, coherence, or composition obstruction.
8. `formal-claim-producer` and canonical proof-v3 only after a later committed
   theory state selects one exact formalization seed.
9. Physics-regime mapping, physical-relevance auditing, and qualitative
   falsification for the final physics return.
10. The canonical N-to-N+8 descendant experiment route only for a
    theory-authored discriminating symbolic or simulation question.

Do not duplicate these skills under new names. Do not invoke historical
fixed-template, caller-plan, metadata-only proof, or same-generation
experiment routes as current authority.

## Entry contract

Require:

- exact target, carrier, regime, quantifiers, protected successes, and
  unresolved conceptual pressure;
- one active verified theory head and its conceptual ancestry;
- the newest admissible bounded literature bundle at a governed use time,
  including retained raw bytes and the semantic bridge;
- a negative-knowledge and retired-route ledger;
- explicit resource, deadline, output, and branch-count ceilings;
- an authored statement of which changes would count as deeper
  understanding, preserved-difference unification, and a physics-relevant
  return; and
- explicit nulls for global latestness, exhaustiveness, source truth,
  creativity, merit, comparative value, theorem status, and physics truth.

Reject a request containing authored candidates, family labels, judge scores,
winners, proof results, experiment results, or a preferred formal language.

## Phase 1: literature-caused open invention

Reopen raw capture, semantic snapshot, bridge, corrections, source versions,
theory head, and governed time transitively. Recheck them after lock, after
each author wave, before aggregation, before commit, and on cache replay.

Commission 8–12 raw author slots before any output exists:

- at least two protected wildcards;
- one capable conservative control when viable;
- multiple mechanism and representation pressures;
- no sibling candidate visibility;
- no supplied corner or family name; and
- identical target/literature access and bounded opportunity.

Run the authors concurrently through the registered killable process wave.
Retain successes, failures, timeouts, malformed outputs, late-write
containment, and exact terminal receipts. Process separation does not establish
provider, model, human, cognitive, or semantic independence.

## Phase 2: family reconstruction and aggressive coverage

Give a separate assessor complete blinded candidate content and the target
literature, but hide author route, provider, status, wildcard/control role, and
self-declared family labels.

Reconstruct each candidate from:

- typed primitive and relation changes;
- explanatory mechanism;
- intervention on a named target path;
- discriminating prediction;
- correction;
- countermodel or first falsifier; and
- exact physics or mathematics return.

Collapse semantic paraphrases and copy/template variants. Keep lexical
lookalikes separate when their typed mechanism, intervention, prediction, or
correction differs.

Require at least six assessor-reconstructed semantic families. An under-six
result is admissible only when a target-predeclared finite constraint model
produces a verifier-replayed UNSAT core or countermodel. Operation counts,
Cartesian products, frozen membership, and producer shortfall are not
satisfiability evidence.

## Phase 3: reflective deepening

Mature every admissible family in isolated branches, then apply the six
conceptual transformations to complete cores:

- ontology: change or defend primitives and identity conditions;
- dialectic: reconstruct the strongest internal contradiction;
- genealogy: expose which historical commitment created the pressure;
- modality: distinguish necessary, contingent, gauge, representational, and
  regime-relative structure;
- unification: find a mediator while preserving decisive differences; and
- philosophy of physics: revise explanatory direction, observability,
  emergence, realism, or theory-world linkage where the target demands it.

Every transformation must state the changed relation, understanding delta,
new burden, correction, discriminating falsifier, and physics handback.
Decorative vocabulary and pure restatement are explicit sham outcomes.

Retain plural incompatible candidates when no evidence justifies convergence.
Preserve negative knowledge even when a candidate is rejected.

## Phase 4: route-neutral semantic evaluation

Preregister nine non-compensatory dimensions:

1. essential relevance;
2. protected-success preservation;
3. explanatory depth;
4. unification with preserved difference;
5. philosophical illumination;
6. falsifiability;
7. correction quality;
8. conceptual risk with asymmetric learning value; and
9. exact physics return.

Run at least three separately configured judges over complete blinded content
with sealed, replayable counterbalanced allocations. Require exact JSON-pointer
and digest warrants, strongest objections, and correction tests.

Include sham, decorative, irrelevant, capable-rival, and copied-status
controls. A capable nonformal rival may win or co-win. Report per-dimension
distributions, uncertainty, agreement, pairwise/best-worst relations, and the
set-valued Pareto frontier.

Any missing judge, incomplete warrant, failed control, allocation splice, or
post-judge rebinding failure produces a typed null with empty preference
authority. Never select the best available malformed result.

## Phase 5: optional Category and HoTT languages

Invoke higher abstraction only when the candidate contains an applicable
multi-representation, mediation, identity, transport, descent, or coherence
pressure.

Run matched Category, HoTT, Category-then-HoTT, HoTT-then-Category, capable
nonformal, and decorative opportunities over identical committed input.

For Category theory require finite objects/carriers, total morphism actions,
identity and composition laws, a nonidentity mediator or explicit obstruction,
exact factorization/naturality scope, protected-observable preservation, and a
candidate-derived physics return.

For HoTT require distinct typed carriers when claiming equivalence, explicit
forward/inverse maps, pointwise round trips, transport scope, indexed
homotopies, exact coherence boundaries, and clear separation of equality,
equivalence, analogy, operational indistinguishability, and univalence.

Compare both orders semantically. An isomorphic relabeling is nonproductive;
a reconstruction ceiling is unavailable; productive noncommutation requires a
verified non-isomorphic conceptual difference with a changed physics burden.

The formal language receives no vocabulary bonus and no theorem, continuum,
univalence, physics-value, or Yang–Mills authority. Use the dedicated YM bridge
only after the generic physics handback exists.

## Phase 6: physics return and subordinate support

For every retained candidate produce:

- phenomenon;
- exact question;
- regime and quantifiers;
- proposed mechanism or explanatory dependency;
- decisive equation, map, or formal burden;
- consequence and protected successes;
- scope and untouched gates;
- correction;
- discriminating falsifier; and
- next theory action.

The candidate must author this content; a shared rule may authorize or deny the
action but may not copy one physics narrative across routes.

Proof is a later source-frozen audit through proof-v3. Symbolic reasoning and
simulation are secondary experiments commissioned only by a committed theory
question. They may expose inconsistency, unit/domain failure, premise
falsification, or information about a live distinction. They cannot alter the
current theory, preference, proof, or schedule in the same generation.

## Mathematical invariants

- Mathematical object: a set-valued frontier of source-bound conceptual cores,
  typed transformations, family relations, formal witnesses where applicable,
  semantic evaluations, and physics-return obligations.
- Preserved invariants: target/carrier/regime/quantifiers, protected successes,
  exact literature ancestry, complete branch evidence, difference-preserving
  unification, plural nondomination, and generation order.
- Broken invariants: taxonomy leakage before invention, copied candidates,
  self-declared families, status-driven judgment, identity/relabel formalism,
  erased physical differences, shared handback text, same-cycle support
  authority, or any unregistered state mutation.
- Counterexamples: include a fluent irrelevant theory, a formal decorative
  relabel, a copied-status candidate, a conservative capable rival, a
  misleading toy positive, a premise falsifier, and an operational failure.

## Output contract

Return one sealed frontier packet containing:

- exact target, theory, literature, code, schema, registry, and time bindings;
- the precommitted author commission and every terminal receipt;
- all raw inventions and blinded family reconstructions;
- verified family coverage or the exact bounded UNSAT/countermodel artifact;
- every maturation and six-lens reflection branch;
- the sealed semantic-evaluation protocol, allocations, reports, controls,
  uncertainty, agreement, and plural outcome;
- every optional Category/HoTT/nonformal/decorative outcome and physics
  handback;
- later proof and descendant-support requests, if any, as nonauthoritative
  children;
- all failures, disagreements, adverse results, negative knowledge, and nulls;
  and
- a next reflective action that preserves the full live candidate set.

Do not publish, bind a target, or claim efficacy merely because this packet
validates.

## Codex exec protocol

1. Inspect the active verified head, target contract, current literature
   authority, conceptual ancestry, negative knowledge, and registered routes.
2. Validate every dependency and schema before author launch.
3. Precommit all author and judge allocations.
4. Execute bounded waves in parallel and retain terminal evidence.
5. Reconstruct families and run copy/semantic attacks before maturation.
6. Run reflection, semantic evaluation, and conditional formal routes.
7. Reopen and rebind every output from immutable bytes before aggregation.
8. Return a plural theory-first packet; commit effects only through the
   canonical single-writer reducer.
9. Run focused deterministic, adversarial, relocation, timeout, and repeated
   replay tests.
10. Run publication-disabled comparative studies before making benefit or
    comparative-value claims.

## Failure and demotion

Fail closed or return a typed null on stale literature, head drift, missing
dependency closure, author leakage, insufficient unverified family coverage,
copy attack, branch loss, nonterminal process evidence, judge/control failure,
rebind mismatch, decorative abstraction, protected-success erasure, copied
physics handback, proof bypass, or descendant experiment authority.

Demote fluent but irrelevant, formally decorative, non-falsifiable, or
correction-free proposals even when they are novel. Preserve their failure as
negative knowledge rather than deleting it.
