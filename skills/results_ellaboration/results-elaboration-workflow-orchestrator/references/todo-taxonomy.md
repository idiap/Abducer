# Result-Elaboration TODO Taxonomy

Each TODO must be self-contained and must use one of these work classes:

- `draft_context`: clarify contribution, non-claim, mathematical object, or section role.
- `proof_completion`: replace a theorem/lemma slot with definitions, statement, proof, and proof-gap audit.
- `coefficient_extraction`: compute or bound `H`, `K`, endpoint terms, color factors, incidence weights, or remainders.
- `same_theory_transfer`: prove that a quotient, normalization, or observable transformation preserves reflected-Gram positivity inside the same regulated theory.
- `finite_application`: apply the finite theorem to a concrete matrix/block and report sign, null sector, lower bound, or obstruction vector.
- `prior_art_boundary`: locate the novelty boundary against known Schur, Feshbach, lattice reflection positivity, Wilson renormalization, heat-kernel, or constructive gauge theory sources.
- `narrative_integration`: update abstract, introduction, section transition, discussion, or appendix explanation after a result is discharged.
- `submission_cleanup`: remove placeholders, author-only material, duplicate labels, or unsupported conditional language.

Required TODO fields:

- source marker and label;
- target paper section;
- mathematical object;
- required inputs;
- action;
- expected artifact;
- acceptance criterion;
- validation command or review handoff;
- stop/quarantine condition;
- narrative consequence.
