# Mixed reflected Wilson-pair trace modulus for OS cone closure

## Candidate Lemma

Mixed reflected collar trace bridge: if fixed-collar reflected quadratic forms are limits of regulated reflection-positive forms and every finite mixed OS matrix entry has a no-new-boundary-renormalization collar trace limit, then the continuum Schwinger functional is OS-positive on the locked gauge-invariant core algebra.

## Definitions

- source object: cutoff gauge-invariant Schwinger functionals S_{a,L} on a fixed positive-time observable algebra, with lattice reflection \Theta, regulated reflection-positive quadratic forms Q_{a,L}(F,c), and Wilson-loop renormalizations chosen before the reflection pairing is formed.
- target object: continuum Schwinger functional S on the same gauge-invariant OS test algebra whose finite matrices M_{ij}=S(\Theta F_i,F_j) are positive semidefinite, so that OS reconstruction can produce a Hilbert space, vacuum vector, and nonnegative time-translation generator.
- controlled interface: regulated reflection-positive Schwinger forms -> OS-positive continuum Schwinger functions via fixed-collar closed-cone passage plus a boundary trace modulus for mixed reflected quadratic forms.
- same-theory condition: the collar map T_\delta and the cutoff removal use the same renormalized observables and embeddings at \delta>0 and \delta=0; no extra reflection-plane counterterm, pair-dependent Wilson renormalization, or algebra enlargement is allowed.
- A_+^{core}: dense positive-time gauge-invariant algebra generated by mirror-flat local composites and admissible renormalized Wilson-loop observables.
- T_\delta: collar-pushing map that moves positive-time observables into t\ge \delta while preserving their labels in the intended continuum algebra.
- mixed OS matrix entry: an entry S(\Theta F_i,F_j), not merely the individual expectation of F_i or F_j; this is where reflected Wilson loops can create hairpin or cusp singularities at t=0.

## Assumptions

- Regulated reflection positivity is valid for every finite family F_i in the cutoff positive-time algebra: Q_{a,L}(F,c)\ge0.
- For each fixed \delta>0, RG/cutoff convergence controls all reflected matrix entries S_{a,L}(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j) for finite families in A_+^{core}.
- The continuum algebra is locked: F_i and T_\delta F_i are representatives of the same intended observable, not different theories related by a boundary renormalization.
- Mixed reflected trace modulus holds entrywise for the finite family: S(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j)\to S(\Theta F_i,F_j) as \delta\downarrow0.
- The core algebra is OS-dense in the final positive-time gauge-invariant test algebra with respect to the seminorms induced by finite OS quadratic forms.

## Equations

- fixed-collar closed cone: `Q_S(T_\delta F,c)=\sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j\,S(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j)\ge0`. Valid when: \delta>0 is fixed, the supports are separated from the reflection plane, the RG topology is valid for these reflected entries, and regulated reflection positivity holds before cutoff removal.
- mixed matrix trace modulus: `\max_{i,j}\left|S(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j)-S(\Theta F_i,F_j)\right|\le \omega_F(\delta),\qquad \omega_F(\delta)\downarrow0`. Valid when: The same renormalized gauge-invariant observable family is used at positive collar and at the plane, with no boundary or pair counterterm.
- positivity after trace: `Q_S(F,c)=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}Q_S(T_\delta F,c)\ge0`. Valid when: The mixed trace modulus is valid for the chosen finite family and coefficients.
- Wilson pair no-defect condition: `D_{C,D}:=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}\left[S(W_R(\Theta C_\delta)W_R(D_\delta))-S(W_R(\Theta C)W_R(D))\right]=0`. Valid when: C and D have admissible mirror-smooth collar limits and the continuum product of Wilson observables has no extra reflection-plane renormalization.

## Attempted Derivation

- Fix a finite family F_1,\ldots,F_N in the positive-time gauge-invariant core and coefficients c_i. For \delta>0, replace F_i by T_\delta F_i so every insertion is separated from the reflection plane.
- At the cutoff level, regulated reflection positivity gives Q_{a,L}(T_\delta F,c)=\sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_jS_{a,L}(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j)\ge0.
- Use the fixed-collar RG convergence topology to pass a,L to the continuum entrywise. Because the cone of positive semidefinite finite matrices is closed, M^\delta_{ij}=S(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j) is positive semidefinite, hence Q_S(T_\delta F,c)\ge0.
- For local mirror-flat composites, prove the trace limit by extending smearings by zero across t=0; the collar translation converges in C_c^\infty, so distributional continuity gives S(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j)\to S(\Theta F_i,F_j). This subcase is valid.
- Try to repeat the same step for Wilson loops. The individual loop labels C_\delta\to C and D_\delta\to D are not enough, because the OS entry involves the product W_R(\Theta C_\delta)W_R(D_\delta). The reflected pair can form a hairpin, cusp, tangency, or contact singularity at t=0 as \delta\downarrow0.
- Introduce the mixed boundary defect B_{ij}=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}M^\delta_{ij}-M_{ij}. If B=0 for all finite Wilson-loop families, then Q_S(F,c)=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}Q_S(T_\delta F,c)\ge0 and the bridge is proved on the Wilson core. If B\ne0, the collar argument proves positivity only for a modified boundary-renormalized matrix, not necessarily for the intended theory.

Blocking gap: No available estimate proves B_{ij}=0 for mixed reflected Wilson-loop entries uniformly through collar collapse. The missing Yang-Mills input is a no-new-boundary-renormalization theorem for every finite OS matrix entry, including pair singularities created only by the product \Theta F_i\cdot F_j.

## Falsifiers

- A concrete falsifier would be a pair of renormalized Wilson loops C,D such that S(W_R(\Theta C_\delta)W_R(D_\delta)) has an uncancelled \log\delta or power divergence as \delta\downarrow0 after all ordinary bulk loop renormalizations.
- A finite nonunit boundary factor B_{C,D}\ne0 is also a falsifier of the same-theory bridge, even if it preserves positivity after redefining a boundary Wilson observable.
- The RG topology may be valid for every fixed collar \delta>0 but fail to provide estimates uniform enough to take \delta\downarrow0 in reflected Wilson pairings.
- Gauge-invariant test algebra mismatch: adding a hairpin counterterm or boundary Wilson-loop generator can make the OS cone closed for a different theory, not for the intended Yang-Mills Schwinger functions.
- Order-of-limits obstruction: if the continuum limit exists only with \delta/a fixed, or if taking a\downarrow0 before \delta\downarrow0 differs from the intended observable at \delta=0, then the bridge fails.

## Learning

Cycle 179 upgrades the obstruction from single-loop trace continuity to finite OS matrix trace continuity. The valid part of the bridge is short: fixed-collar reflection positivity passes by closedness, and local mirror-flat composites pass to the plane by distributional continuity. The hard Yang-Mills question is whether mixed reflected Wilson products acquire a boundary defect as \Theta C_\delta and D_\delta collide at t=0. A nonzero mixed defect is a same-theory falsifier: it may still leave a positive boundary-renormalized theory, but it does not prove OS positivity for the intended continuum Schwinger functions.
