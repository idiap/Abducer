## Workflow

1. Extract the strongest current claims from the spec, execution log, and test evidence.
2. Check whether the evidence really supports those claims.
3. Surface hidden assumptions, untested premises, or scope leaps.
4. Identify missing falsification checks and missing boundary conditions.
5. Turn the most important issues into concrete TODOs for the persona-review artifact.

## Guardrails

- Prefer evidence-backed criticism over philosophical mood-setting.
- Distinguish absence of evidence from contradictory evidence.
- Name uncertainty explicitly.

## Output Contract

Produce:

- strongest supported claims
- under-justified or overstated claims
- missing falsification or boundary checks
- concrete TODOs
