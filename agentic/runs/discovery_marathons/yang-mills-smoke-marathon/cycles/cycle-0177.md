# Reflection-Positivity Closed-Cone Topology: Wilson-Loop Collar Trace Test

## Candidate Lemma

Wilson-loop collar trace bridge: OS positivity for the continuum gauge-invariant Schwinger functional extends from the fixed-collar algebra to the full positive-time algebra including Wilson loops touching the reflection plane, provided the cutoff-removal topology controls all fixed-collar reflected Wilson-loop quadratic forms and the renormalized Wilson-loop sector has a no-new-boundary-renormalization trace modulus as the collar width tends to zero.

## Definitions

- source object: a net of regulated reflection-positive gauge-invariant Schwinger functionals S_{a,L} on cutoff algebras generated by local gauge-invariant composites and canonically renormalized Wilson-loop observables W^R_{a}(C), with positive-time support at distance at least delta from the reflection plane for the collar subalgebra.
- target object: a continuum Schwinger functional S on the locked gauge-invariant test algebra, with OS quadratic form Q_S(F,c)=\sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j S(\Theta F_i,F_j) and the reconstructed Hilbert-space inner product obtained by quotienting null vectors.
- controlled interface: collar maps T_delta from positive-time observables to observables supported in x_0>=delta, together with RG convergence of every finite reflected quadratic form Q_{a,L}(T_delta F,c) to Q_S(T_delta F,c).
- same-theory condition: W^R(C) is identified with lim_{delta downarrow 0} W^R(T_delta C) in the same continuum Wilson-loop algebra, with no added reflection-plane counterterm, no extra cusp normalization, and no change of gauge-invariant observable quotient.
- The collar algebra A^+_delta is the subalgebra generated by observables whose Euclidean-time support has distance at least delta from the reflection plane x_0=0.
- A Wilson-loop trace modulus is a function omega_{F,c}(delta) tending to zero such that |Q_S(T_delta F,c)-Q_S(F,c)|<=omega_{F,c}(delta) for every finite family F=(F_1,...,F_n) in the Wilson-loop polynomial algebra.

## Assumptions

- For every cutoff pair (a,L), the regulated lattice or continuum-regularized gauge-invariant functional is reflection positive on the positive-time algebra: Q_{a,L}(G,c)>=0 whenever G_i are supported in x_0>0.
- For each fixed delta>0 and finite Wilson-loop polynomial family F, the cutoff-removal topology is strong enough that Q_{a,L}(T_delta F,c) converges to Q_S(T_delta F,c).
- The collar maps T_delta preserve the gauge-invariant observable labels in the continuum algebra: they are approximating embeddings, not a gauge fixing or a boundary-condition change.
- There is a trace modulus for the renormalized Wilson-loop sector, after subtracting only the canonical bulk perimeter and pre-existing cusp renormalizations already used away from the reflection plane.
- The continuum Schwinger functional is sequentially complete for the OS seminorm limits used by the collar approximation.

## Equations

- fixed-collar regulated positivity: `Q_{a,L}^{W,\delta}(F,c)=\sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j\,S_{a,L}(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j)\ge 0`. Valid when: Each T_delta F_i belongs to the positive-time cutoff gauge-invariant algebra and the regulator is reflection positive before cutoff removal.
- fixed-collar closed-cone transfer: `Q_{a,L}^{W,\delta}(F,c)\to Q_{S}^{W,\delta}(F,c):=\sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j\,S(\Theta T_\delta F_i,T_\delta F_j)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Q_S^{W,\delta}(F,c)\ge0`. Valid when: The RG topology is valid for the particular reflected Wilson-loop probes at fixed positive collar distance delta.
- Wilson-loop trace modulus: `\left|Q_S^{W,\delta}(F,c)-Q_S^{W,0}(F,c)\right|\le \omega_{F,c}(\delta),\qquad \omega_{F,c}(\delta)\downarrow0`. Valid when: Renormalized Wilson-loop Schwinger functions are continuous under collar collapse and no reflection-plane boundary renormalization is introduced.
- residual boundary-renormalization test: `B_a(C,\delta):=\frac{Z_a^{\mathrm{can}}(T_\delta C)}{Z_a^{\mathrm{can}}(C)}\,\exp\{-m_a(\ell(T_\delta C)-\ell(C))\}\,\frac{\prod_{v\in C}Z_{\mathrm{cusp},a}(\theta_v(C))}{\prod_{v\in T_\delta C}Z_{\mathrm{cusp},a}(\theta_v(T_\delta C))},\qquad \lim_{\delta\downarrow0}\limsup_{a\downarrow0}|B_a(C,\delta)-1|=0`. Valid when: Canonical Wilson-loop renormalization factorizes into bulk perimeter and ordinary cusp factors away from the reflection plane; B_a isolates only a possible new mirror-boundary factor.

## Attempted Derivation

- Fix a finite family F_1,...,F_n of positive-time gauge-invariant Wilson-loop polynomials and coefficients c_i. For delta>0, replace each F_i by T_delta F_i supported strictly in x_0>=delta.
- At each regulator, reflection positivity gives Q_{a,L}(T_delta F,c)>=0. This step is valid only for the locked gauge-invariant cutoff algebra; if T_delta requires gauge fixing or a boundary condition, the argument has already changed theories.
- Use the fixed-collar RG convergence to pass a,L to the continuum: Q_S(T_delta F,c)>=0 for every fixed delta>0. This is the already-understood closed-cone part.
- To remove the collar, try to write Q_S(F,c)=lim_{delta downarrow 0}Q_S(T_delta F,c). For local gauge-invariant composites smeared by functions flat at the reflection plane, this follows from ordinary distributional continuity.
- For Wilson loops, replace distributional continuity by the trace modulus. The needed estimate is uniform over the finitely many reflected loop products appearing in the OS quadratic form and must be stated after the canonical bulk Wilson-loop renormalization.
- If the trace modulus holds, then Q_S(F,c)=lim_delta Q_S(T_delta F,c)>=0, proving OS positivity for the desired continuum Wilson-loop observables.
- The derivation fails if collar collapse creates a residual cusp or boundary factor: then the limit of Q_S(T_delta F,c) is positive, but it is the quadratic form of a boundary-renormalized observable rather than Q_S(F,c).

Blocking gap: No available Yang-Mills estimate currently proves the Wilson-loop trace modulus uniformly through reflection-plane collar collapse; the precise gap is exclusion of residual mirror-boundary/cusp renormalization in reflected Wilson-loop correlators.

## Falsifiers

- falsifier: a reflected hairpin Wilson loop whose collar collapse creates a nontrivial cusp anomalous factor not canceled by the canonical away-from-plane Wilson-loop renormalization.
- falsifier: two collar families T_delta C and T'_delta C approaching the same loop C but giving different limits for Q_S(Theta W^R(T_delta C),W^R(T_delta C)).
- falsifier: RG compactness bounds that are valid for every fixed delta>0 but have constants blowing up as delta downarrow 0 so that no trace modulus can be extracted.
- falsifier: a construction in which loops touching the reflection plane are defined by adding a boundary counterterm or new boundary generator; positivity may hold, but it is not the same-theory continuum Yang-Mills algebra.
- falsifier: failure of OS-seminorm density of the mirror-smooth gauge-invariant core, leaving a positive-norm sector of the intended algebra outside the collar argument.

## Learning

Cycle 177 identifies the Wilson-loop sector as the exact place where the OS bridge is either proved or falsified. The valid closed-cone argument gives positivity for every fixed collar, and local mirror-flat composites pass to the plane by distributional continuity. For Wilson loops, however, collar collapse is a renormalization question: a residual boundary or cusp factor would be a falsifier of the same-theory condition even if positivity survives in a modified algebra. The sharpened bridge is therefore: prove a no-new-boundary-renormalization trace modulus for mirror-smooth Wilson-loop collars, then extend by OS density, or exhibit a concrete hairpin/cusp counterexample.
